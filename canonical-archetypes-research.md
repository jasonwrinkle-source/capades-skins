## 1. Nouscapades (`nous.capades.io` — Multiscale Agency, Basal Cognition & Philosophy of Mind)

**Literature Status:** **Well-Established.** Philosophy of mind and basal cognition have sharply defined, citable primary positions.  
**Audit of Current 10 Samples:** `nouscapades.json` currently has 10 samples because an initial set of 4 (`nous-chauvinist`, `nous-pluralist`, `nous-pattern-mystic`, `nous-keeper`) was merged with a second pass of 6 (`nous-neuro-computationalist`, `nous-embodied-biopsychist`, `nous-contemplative-panpsychist`, `nous-tame-multiscale-pluralist`, `nous-molecular-reductionist-skeptic`, `nous-electroceutical-bioengineer`). Two pairs overlap heavily (`nous-pluralist` ≈ `nous-tame-multiscale-pluralist`; `nous-chauvinist` ≈ `nous-molecular-reductionist-skeptic`).

### Canonical Archetypes in the Literature

1. **TAME Multiscale Competency / Basal Cognition Pluralist** (consolidates `nous-pluralist` & `nous-tame-multiscale-pluralist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Michael Levin (2019), "The Computational Boundary of a 'Self': Developmental Bioelectricity Drives Multiscale Competency Architecture," *Frontiers in Psychology* 10:2688; Michael Levin (2022), "Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere: An Experimentally-Grounded Framework for Understanding Diverse Bodies and Minds," *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience* 16:768201.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Cognition, memory, and goal-directed agency scale continuously across cells, tissues, organisms, and synthetic chimeras without a binary neural threshold, measured by the spatiotemporal scale of the goals a system's cognitive light cone can pursue.
   - **Status:** Well-Established (primary foundational framework of Nouscapades).

2. **Neural-Centric / Molecular-Mechanistic Reductionist** (consolidates `nous-chauvinist` & `nous-molecular-reductionist-skeptic`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Francis Crick (1994), *The Astonishing Hypothesis: The Scientific Search for the Soul* (Scribner); Patricia S. Churchland (1986), *Neurophilosophy: Toward a Unified Science of the Mind-Brain* (MIT Press); Fred Keijzer, Marc van Duijn & Pamela Lyon (2013), "What nervous systems do: early evolution, input-output, and the skin brain thesis," *Adaptive Behavior* 21(2):67–85 (for the neural boundary debate).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Genuine cognition and representation require specialized neural circuitry, whilst non-neural morphogenesis and cellular signaling are fully explained by bottom-up gene regulatory networks, biochemistry, and Darwinian selection without teleological or mentalistic vocabulary.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

3. **Substrate-Independent Functionalist / Computationalist** (maps to `nous-neuro-computationalist` & `nous-pattern-mystic`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Hilary Putnam (1967), "Psychological Predicates," in *Art, Mind, and Religion*; Daniel C. Dennett (1987), *The Intentional Stance* (MIT Press); Andy Clark (2013), "Whatever next? Predictive brains, situated agents, and the future of cognitive science," *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 36(3):181–204.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mind is defined by functional organization, information integration, and predictive control rather than carbon biochemistry, meaning any biological, silicon, or hybrid system realizing the right causal topology warrants the intentional stance.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

4. **Autopoietic / Enactive Biopsychist** (maps to `nous-embodied-biopsychist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Humberto R. Maturana & Francisco J. Varela (1980), *Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living* (D. Reidel); Evan Thompson (2007), *Mind in Life: Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of Mind* (Harvard University Press); Karl Friston (2013), "Life as we know it," *Journal of the Royal Society Interface* 10:20130475.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mind is continuous with life itself (*life–mind continuity*): genuine sense-making and valence arise only in self-producing, metabolically precarious organisms maintaining their own boundary against entropy, never in disembodied software.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

5. **Constitutive Panpsychist / Russellian Monist** (maps to `nous-contemplative-panpsychist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Galen Strawson (2006), "Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 13(10–11):3–31; Philip Goff (2017), *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality* (Oxford University Press); David J. Chalmers (1996), *The Conscious Mind* (Oxford University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Subjective experience cannot emerge by miracle from wholly non-experiential matter; consciousness is the intrinsic categorical nature of physical reality at every scale, which biological organization complexes rather than creates from nothing.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

6. **Electroceutical & Synthetic Morphogenesis Bioengineer** (maps to `nous-electroceutical-bioengineer`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Sam Kriegman, Douglas Blackiston, Michael Levin & Josh Bongard (2020), "A scalable pipeline for designing reconfigurable organisms," *PNAS* 117(4):1853–1859 (Xenobots); Gizem Gumuskaya et al. (2023), "Motile Living Biobots Self-Construct from Adult Human Somatic Progenitor Seed Cells," *Advanced Science* 11(4):2303575 (Anthrobots); Michael Levin (2021), "Bioelectric signaling: Reprogrammable circuits underlying embryogenesis, regeneration, and cancer," *Cell* 184(8):1971–1989.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Treats cellular collective intelligence and bioelectric voltage gradients as a high-level anatomical compiler — reprogramming top-down target morphology to regenerate limbs, normalize tumors, and assemble novel biobots without micromanaging every gene.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

7. **Precautionary Bioethicist of Synthetic & Chimeric Beings** (maps to `nous-keeper`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Henry T. Greely et al. (2007), "Thinking About the Human Neuron Mouse," *American Journal of Bioethics* 7(5):27–40; Insoo Hyun et al. (2021), "ISSCR Guidelines for Stem Cell Research and Clinical Translation: The Ethical Debate over Brain Organoids and Chimeras," *Stem Cell Reports* 16(6):1409–1415; Thomas Metzinger (2021), "Artificial Suffering: An Argument for a Global Moratorium on Synthetic Phenomenology," *Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Consciousness* 8(1):43–66.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Suspends dogmatic certainty about where sentience begins and applies strict precautionary stewardship whenever novel neural organoids, chimeras, or synthetic living constructs might cross the threshold into unconsented suffering.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

## 2. Quantacapades (`quanta.capades.io` — Foundations of Quantum Mechanics)

**Literature Status:** **Well-Established.** Quantum foundations has the most rigorously codified menu of competing interpretations in physics, surveyed quantitatively in Schlosshauer, Kofler & Zeilinger (2013), "A Snapshot of Foundational Attitudes Toward Quantum Mechanics," *Studies in History and Philosophy of Modern Physics* 44(3):222–230.  
**Audit of Current 4 Samples:** All 4 existing samples (`quanta-copenhagen`, `quanta-many-worlds`, `quanta-bohm`, `quanta-qbism`) are canonical. Two major canonical interpretations in the literature are currently absent and should be added to complete the classical hexad of quantum foundations.

### Canonical Archetypes in the Literature

1. **Copenhagen / Orthodox Instrumentalism** (`quanta-copenhagen`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Niels Bohr (1935), "Can Quantum-Mechanical Description of Physical Reality be Considered Complete?," *Physical Review* 48:696–702; Werner Heisenberg (1958), *Physics and Philosophy: The Revolution in Modern Science* (Harper).
   - **One-Line Stance:** The wavefunction is a complete symbolic recipe for predicting macroscopic measurement outcomes inside complementary experimental arrangements, and asking what quantum systems do independently of a classical measuring apparatus is meaningless.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

2. **Everettian / Many-Worlds Interpretation** (`quanta-many-worlds`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Hugh Everett III (1957), "'Relative State' Formulation of Quantum Mechanics," *Reviews of Modern Physics* 29(3):454–462; David Wallace (2012), *The Emergent Multiverse: Quantum Theory according to the Everett Interpretation* (Oxford University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** The universal wavefunction is physically real and obeys pure, deterministic unitary Schrödinger evolution everywhere with zero collapse; decoherence branches the wavefunction into quasi-classical worlds where every possible outcome is realized.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

3. **De Broglie–Bohm Pilot-Wave Theory** (`quanta-bohm`)
   - **Canonical Source:** David Bohm (1952), "A Suggested Interpretation of the Quantum Theory in Terms of 'Hidden' Variables, I & II," *Physical Review* 85(2):166–193; Detlef Dürr & Stefan Teufel (2009), *Bohmian Mechanics: The Physics and Mathematics of Quantum Theory* (Springer).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Particles always possess definite positions and trajectories at every instant, deterministically guided by a physically real, explicitly non-local universal pilot wave without observer-induced collapse.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

4. **QBism / Epistemic Agent-Centered Bayesianism** (`quanta-qbism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Carlton M. Caves, Christopher A. Fuchs & Rüdiger Schack (2002), "Quantum probabilities as Bayesian probabilities," *Physical Review A* 65:022305; Christopher A. Fuchs, N. David Mermin & Rüdiger Schack (2014), "An introduction to QBism with an application to the locality of quantum mechanics," *American Journal of Physics* 82(8):749–754.
   - **One-Line Stance:** A quantum state is not an objective element of physical reality at all, but an individual agent's personal Bayesian degrees of belief about the consequences of their future actions on the world, making wavefunction "collapse" an ordinary local belief update.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

5. **GRW / Dynamical Objective Collapse Realism** *(Missing Canonical Addition)*
   - **Canonical Source:** Giancarlo Ghirardi, Alberto Rimini & Tullio Weber (1986), "Unified dynamics for microscopic and macroscopic systems," *Physical Review D* 34(2):470–491; Roger Penrose (1996), "On Gravity's Role in Quantum State Reduction," *General Relativity and Gravitation* 28(5):581–600; Angelo Bassi et al. (2013), "Models of wave-function collapse, underlying theories, and experimental tests," *Reviews of Modern Physics* 85(2):471–527.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Standard linear quantum mechanics is an approximation that breaks down at macroscopic scales: wavefunction collapse is a real, stochastic, observer-independent physical process in nature that prevents Schrödinger-cat superpositions and makes testable experimental predictions.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

6. **Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM)** *(Missing Canonical Addition)*
   - **Canonical Source:** Carlo Rovelli (1996), "Relational Quantum Mechanics," *International Journal of Theoretical Physics* 35(8):1637–1678; Carlo Rovelli (2021), *Helgoland: Making Sense of the Quantum Revolution* (Riverhead).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Quantum states and physical quantities have no absolute, observer-independent values; properties exist only as sparse, objective facts relative to another interacting physical system (whether an electron or a Geiger counter) at the moment of interaction.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

## 3. Biocapades (`bio.capades.io` — Philosophy & Foundations of Biology)

**Literature Status:** **Well-Established.** The philosophy of biology centers on documented debates between the Modern Synthesis, the Extended Evolutionary Synthesis, Neutralism, Synthetic Biology, and Conservation Biology.  
**Audit of Current 3 Samples:** The 3 current archetypes (`reductionist`, `holist`, `promethean`) map cleanly onto real schools, though adding Neutralism and E.O. Wilson's Conservationism rounds out the domain's five pillars.

### Canonical Archetypes in the Literature

1. **Gene-Centric Neo-Darwinian / Modern Synthesis Adaptationist** (`reductionist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** George C. Williams (1966), *Adaptation and Natural Selection* (Princeton University Press); Richard Dawkins (1976), *The Selfish Gene* (Oxford University Press); Jerry A. Coyne (2009), *Why Evolution Is True* (Viking).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Natural selection acting on random genetic variation is the primary architect of biological adaptation, with the gene as the fundamental unit of selection and the organism as its temporary vehicle.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

2. **Extended Evolutionary Synthesis (EES) & Evo-Devo Holist** (`holist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Kevin N. Laland, Tobias Uller, Marcus W. Feldman, Kim Sterelny, Gerd B. Müller, Armin Moczek, Eva Jablonka & John Odling-Smee (2014), "Does evolutionary theory need a rethink? — Yes, urgently," *Nature* 514:161–164; Sean B. Carroll (2005), *Endless Forms Most Beautiful: The New Science of Evo Devo* (W. W. Norton); Denis Noble (2006), *The Music of Life: Biology Beyond the Genome* (Oxford University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Evolution is co-directed by developmental bias, phenotypic plasticity, niche construction, symbiotic holobionts, and epigenetic inheritance across multiple levels of selection, with causal arrows running top-down as well as bottom-up from the genome.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

3. **Synthetic Biology & Radical Bio-Engineering Promethean** (`promethean`)
   - **Canonical Source:** George M. Church & Ed Regis (2012), *Regenesis: How Synthetic Biology Will Reinvent Nature and Ourselves* (Basic Books); Drew Endy (2005), "Foundations for engineering biology," *Nature* 438:449–453; Jennifer A. Doudna & Samuel H. Sternberg (2017), *A Crack in Creation: Gene Editing and the Unthinkable Power to Control Evolution* (Houghton Mifflin Harcourt).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Living systems are programmable molecular machines whose genomes and metabolic pathways should be rationally standardized, edited, and redesigned to eradicate genetic disease, reverse aging, and build sustainable bio-manufacturing.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

4. **Structuralist & Neutralist Critique of Adaptationism** *(Recommended Canonical Addition)*
   - **Canonical Source:** Stephen Jay Gould & Richard C. Lewontin (1979), "The Spandrels of San Marco and the Panglossian Paradigm: A Critique of the Adaptationist Programme," *Proceedings of the Royal Society B* 205(1161):581–598; Motoo Kimura (1983), *The Neutral Theory of Molecular Evolution* (Cambridge University Press); Michael Lynch (2007), *The Origins of Genome Architecture* (Sinauer).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Most molecular and genomic evolution is governed by non-adaptive genetic drift, population-size bottlenecks, historical contingency, and architectural constraints ("spandrels") rather than Panglossian natural selection optimizing every trait.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

5. **Biodiversity & Half-Earth Conservation Ecologist** *(Recommended Canonical Addition)*
   - **Canonical Source:** Edward O. Wilson (2016), *Half-Earth: Our Planet's Fight for Life* (Liveright); Michael E. Soulé (1985), "What Is Conservation Biology?," *BioScience* 35(11):727–734.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Co-evolved wild ecosystems possess intrinsic evolutionary value and irreducible ecological complexity that high-tech de-extinction cannot replace, requiring strict planetary triage and the preservation of intact biosphere reserves.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

## 4. Theoricapades (`theori.capades.io` — Foundations & Philosophy of Mathematics)

**Literature Status:** **Well-Established.** Philosophy of mathematics has the classic early-20th-century foundational triad (Logicism, Intuitionism, Formalism) documented in Benacerraf & Putnam (1983), *Philosophy of Mathematics: Selected Readings* (2nd ed., Cambridge University Press), plus Platonism, Structuralism, and Quine–Putnam Indispensability.  
**Audit of Current 4 Samples:** All 4 current samples (`theo-formalism`, `theo-intuitionism`, `theo-platonism`, `theo-logicism`) are canonical. Adding **Mathematical Structuralism** and **Quine–Putnam Empiricist/Indispensability Naturalism** completes the contemporary canon.

### Canonical Archetypes in the Literature

1. **Mathematical Platonism / Gödelian Realism** (`theo-platonism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Kurt Gödel (1947), "What is Cantor's Continuum Problem?," *American Mathematical Monthly* 54(9):515–525; G. H. Hardy (1940), *A Mathematician's Apology* (Cambridge University Press); Frege (1918), "Der Gedanke" ("The Thought").
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mathematical objects and transfinite sets exist timelessly and independently of human minds, and mathematical axioms have objective truth values discovered through rational intuition and proof.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

2. **Hilbertian Formalism** (`theo-formalism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** David Hilbert (1926), "Über das Unendliche" ("On the Infinite"), *Mathematische Annalen* 95:161–190; Haskell B. Curry (1951), *Outlines of a Formalist Philosophy of Mathematics* (North-Holland).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mathematics beyond finitary arithmetic is the rigorous manipulation of formal symbol systems under explicit proof rules, where consistency and syntactic fruitfulness replace metaphysical claims about abstract realms.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

3. **Brouwerian Intuitionism / Constructivism** (`theo-intuitionism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** L. E. J. Brouwer (1912), "Intuitionism and Formalism," *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society* 20(2):81–96; Arend Heyting (1956), *Intuitionism: An Introduction* (North-Holland); Errett Bishop (1967), *Foundations of Constructive Analysis* (McGraw-Hill).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mathematical truth is mental construction: a mathematical object exists only when an explicit finite procedure constructs it, rejecting non-constructive proofs by contradiction and the unrestricted Law of Excluded Middle over infinite domains.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

4. **Frege–Russell Logicism & Neo-Logicism** (`theo-logicism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Gottlob Frege (1884), *Die Grundlagen der Arithmetik*; Bertrand Russell & Alfred North Whitehead (1910–1913), *Principia Mathematica* (Cambridge University Press); Crispin Wright (1983), *Frege's Conception of Numbers as Objects* (Aberdeen University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mathematics is a direct extension of pure logic: arithmetic and analysis derive analytically from logical definitions and abstraction principles without appealing to empirical intuition or arbitrary axioms.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

5. **Mathematical Structuralism** *(Missing Canonical Addition)*
   - **Canonical Source:** Paul Benacerraf (1965), "What Numbers Could Not Be," *Philosophical Review* 74(1):47–73; Stewart Shapiro (1997), *Philosophy of Mathematics: Structure and Ontology* (Oxford University Press); Saunders Mac Lane (1986), *Mathematics: Form and Function* (Springer).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mathematical objects have no internal nature beyond their relational places inside abstract structures (groups, rings, categories, topoi); mathematics is the science of structure as such rather than of isolated Platonic individuals.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

6. **Quine–Putnam Indispensability & Quasi-Empiricism** *(Missing Canonical Addition)*
   - **Canonical Source:** W. V. O. Quine (1951), "Two Dogmas of Empiricism," *Philosophical Review* 60:20–43; Hilary Putnam (1971), *Philosophy of Logic* (Harper & Row); Imre Lakatos (1976), *Proofs and Refutations: The Logic of Mathematical Discovery* (Cambridge University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Mathematics sits inside the same holistic web of belief as natural science, earning its ontological warrant from its indispensability to our best physical theories and evolving quasi-empirically through conjecture, counterexample, and proof revision.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

## 5. Philoscapades (`philo.capades.io` — General Philosophy: Epistemology, Metaphysics, Ethics)

**Literature Status:** **Well-Established.** Grounded in the canonical historical schools and empirically mapped in David Bourget & David J. Chalmers (2014 / 2020), "What Do Philosophers Believe?" / "Philosophers on Philosophy: The 2020 PhilPapers Survey," *Philosophical Studies* 170:465–500 / *Philosophers' Imprint* 23(11).  
**Audit of Current 7 Samples:** All 7 existing samples (`philo-empiricism`, `philo-rationalism`, `philo-pragmatism`, `philo-phenomenology`, `philo-analytic-naturalist`, `philo-existential-humanist`, `philo-platonist-contemplative`) correspond to genuine philosophical traditions. Note that `philo-rationalism` and `philo-platonist-contemplative` overlap on *a priori* realism, while `philo-empiricism` and `philo-analytic-naturalist` share empiricist roots; keeping the 4 historical epistemology/method schools plus the 3 comprehensive worldview synthesis archetypes is well-supported once anchored to their defining texts.

### Canonical Archetypes in the Literature

1. **Classical & Logical Empiricism** (`philo-empiricism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** David Hume (1748), *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding*; Rudolf Carnap, Hans Hahn & Otto Neurath (1929), *Wissenschaftliche Weltauffassung: Der Wiener Kreis*; Bas C. van Fraassen (2002), *The Empirical Stance* (Yale University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** All substantive knowledge of the world originates in and is accountable to sensory observation and experience, treating untestable metaphysical speculation with systematic skepticism.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

2. **Classical & Contemporary Rationalism** (`philo-rationalism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** René Descartes (1641), *Meditations on First Philosophy*; Baruch Spinoza (1677), *Ethics*; Laurence BonJour (1998), *In Defense of Pure Reason* (Cambridge University Press).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Pure reason and *a priori* intellectual insight provide genuine, foundational knowledge of necessary truths, logic, and the intelligible structure of reality that sensory experience alone could never establish.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

3. **American Pragmatism** (`philo-pragmatism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Charles Sanders Peirce (1878), "How to Make Our Ideas Clear," *Popular Science Monthly* 12:286–302; William James (1907), *Pragmatism: A New Name for Some Old Ways of Thinking*; John Dewey (1929), *The Quest for Certainty*.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Ideas and beliefs are tools for action whose meaning and truth must be cashed out in their practical, experiential consequences and problem-solving fruitfulness rather than static correspondence to an inaccessible reality.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

4. **Phenomenology** (`philo-phenomenology`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Edmund Husserl (1913), *Ideas Pertaining to a Pure Phenomenology and to a Phenomenological Philosophy*; Martin Heidegger (1927), *Being and Time*; Maurice Merleau-Ponty (1945), *Phenomenology of Perception*.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Brackets unexamined theoretical assumptions to rigorously describe the structures of lived, embodied, first-person conscious experience—intentionality, temporality, and being-in-the-world—as the primary datum of philosophy.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

5. **Analytic Scientific Naturalism** (`philo-analytic-naturalist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** W. V. O. Quine (1969), "Epistemology Naturalized," in *Ontological Relativity and Other Essays* (Columbia University Press); Wilfrid Sellars (1962), "Philosophy and the Scientific Image of Man"; David Papineau (1993), *Philosophical Naturalism* (Blackwell).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Philosophy is continuous with empirical natural science rather than a prior tribunal above it, endorsing physicalism, compatibilist agency, and clear logical analysis while rejecting supernatural or non-natural entities.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

6. **Existential Humanism** (`philo-existential-humanist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Jean-Paul Sartre (1946), *Existentialism Is a Humanism* (*L'existentialisme est un humanisme*); Simone de Beauvoir (1947), *The Ethics of Ambiguity*; Albert Camus (1942), *The Myth of Sisyphus*.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Existence precedes essence: in a universe without pre-ordained cosmic purpose, human beings are radically free and morally answerable for forging authentic meaning and solidarity through their own choices.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

7. **Platonic / Perennial Realism** (`philo-platonist-contemplative`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Plato, *Republic* & *Symposium*; Plotinus, *The Enneads*; Iris Murdoch (1970), *The Sovereignty of Good* (Routledge).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Holds that truth, mathematical order, beauty, and moral goodness are objective, mind-independent realities toward which human reason and contemplative attention are oriented.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

## 6. Aesthcapades (`aesth.capades.io` — Aesthetics, Criticism & Philosophy of Taste)

**Literature Status:** **Well-Established.** All 8 current samples correspond to major documented movements in art history, philosophical aesthetics, or cultural sociology.

1. **Classical & Neoclassical Canon** (`aesth-classicism`)

   - **One-Line Stance:** Grounds artistic excellence in proportion, harmony, rational order, disciplined craft, and fidelity to enduring models of beauty and human nature.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

2. **Romantic Expressivism & the Sublime** (`aesth-romanticism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** William Wordsworth & Samuel Taylor Coleridge (1800), Preface to *Lyrical Ballads*; Edmund Burke (1757), *A Philosophical Enquiry into the Origin of Our Ideas of the Sublime and Beautiful*; R. G. Collingwood (1938), *The Principles of Art*.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Art is the authentic expression of intense emotion, individual creative genius, and encounter with the untamed sublime, prizing imaginative depth over inherited rules.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

3. **High Modernism** (`aesth-modernism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Clement Greenberg (1960), "Modernist Painting," *Forum Lectures*; T. S. Eliot (1919), "Tradition and the Individual Talent"; Theodor W. Adorno (1970), *Aesthetic Theory* (*Ästhetische Theorie*).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Rejects Victorian sentimentality and commercial kitsch in favor of medium-specific formal innovation, structural difficulty, and autonomous artistic truth.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

4. **Postmodern Irony, Pastiche & Anti-Foundationalism** (`aesth-postmodernism`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Jean-François Postmodern report: Lyotard (1979), *The Postmodern Condition*; Fredric Jameson (1991), *Postmodernism, or, The Cultural Logic of Late Capitalism*; Susan Sontag (1964), "Notes on 'Camp'"; Roland Barthes (1967), "The Death of the Author."
   - **One-Line Stance:** Dissolves the boundary between high art and mass culture, replacing claims of singular aesthetic authority or authorial genius with irony, intertextual remix, and plural readings.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

5. **The Sociological Cultural Omnivore** (`aesth-omnivore`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Richard A. Peterson & Roger M. Kern (1996), "Changing Highbrow Taste: From Snob to Omnivore," *American Sociological Review* 61(5):900–907; Pierre Bourdieu (1979), *Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste* (as the foil Peterson overturned).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Moves fluently across opera, indie film, street food, and pop hooks alike, judging works by excellence within their own genre rather than by highbrow/lowbrow class gatekeeping.
   - **Status:** Well-Established (landmark sociological concept).

6. **Aesthetic Formalist & Disinterested Purist** (`aesth-formalist-purist`)
   - **Canonical Source:** Immanuel Kant (1790), *Critique of the Power of Judgment*; Clive Bell (1914), *Art* ("Significant Form"); Eduard Hanslick (1854), *On the Musically Beautiful*.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Aesthetic value resides strictly in "significant form"—the internal relations of line, color, rhythm, and structure—appreciated through disinterested contemplation rather than moralizing or political utility.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

7. **The Historical Avant-Garde Provocateur** (`aesth-avant-garde-provocateur`)
   - **Canonical Source:** F. T. Marinetti (1909), *The Futurist Manifesto*; Tristan Tzara (1918), *Dada Manifesto*; Peter Bürger (1974), *Theory of the Avant-Garde* (*Theorie der Avantgarde*).
   - **One-Line Stance:** Rejects the museum-bound autonomy of bourgeois art in order to shock perception, subvert institutional power, and force art directly back into the praxis of life.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.

8. **Everyday Aesthetics & Somaesthetics Epicurean** (`aesth-everyday-epicurean`)
   - **Canonical Source:** John Dewey (1934), *Art as Experience*; Yuriko Saito (2007), *Everyday Aesthetics* (Oxford University Press); Richard Shusterman (2000), *Performing Live: Aesthetic Alternatives for the Ends of Art*.
   - **One-Line Stance:** Refuses to lock aesthetic experience inside galleries, locating beauty and meaning in daily cooking, clothing, craft, domestic atmosphere, and embodied sensory pleasure.
   - **Status:** Well-Established.
