# Flex — Research

Skin: Flexcapades (`flexcapades.json`) · Domain: flex.capades.io · Meter: stake
Coverage: 32 topics, 94 poles, 7 archetypes (all counts taken from the skin JSON).

## 1. Domain framing

Flex is the home skin: the map of Flexcapades itself. Its domain, in its own words, is "how you use Flexcapades, and how you want it to grow: the features and flows of the app itself — plus the open questions the project is still deciding, where the room gets its say." It is the only skin whose subject is the instrument every other skin is played on, and its stance follows from that: the app is not finished doctrine handed down to users, but a shared tool whose users hold a stake in its shape. A position here is one of two kinds, and the skin keeps both on one map deliberately: a rated usage pattern (where you actually rate, what you actually open, how you actually keep and guard your profile) or a rated say on an open project question (roster hosting, the guide's version, the DJ view, the next skin). Holding no stance is not a position; a 0 rating is. The meter word is **stake**: the skin measures how much of a stake a profile has in each pole — advocacy per pole, in this skin's own terms leaning and appetite as its topics describe them — and how open it is to each topic at all (openness, per topic). Receptivity and intensity are not rated directly — receptivity is derived as round-half-even(openness × advocacy) computed with exact fractions, and intensity is the signed largest-|value| pole, derived from the poles. The Scribe is an indaba: it uses this research to show each position with its backing, name where holders meet and part, and ask what would let the group reach consensus — for this skin, that means the room's usage and the room's wishes can be talked toward a shared way of running the app and a shared direction for building it.

## 3. Reasons and statistics backing each position

Every statistic field in this section reads `No reliable statistic found.` — deliberately. This skin maps one specific app's use and its project's open questions; there is no published, independent usage research on Flexcapades to cite, and inventing adoption numbers for the app's own features would be exactly the fabrication the research rules forbid. The reasons are the backing here.

## 5. Gap analysis

**Positions with no archetype.** The map's poles are wider than its 7 archetypes. No archetype primarily embodies: the Minus-first indaba convenor (the Lawgiver rules the map but is not defined by the feud view); the Sentinel operator who runs timed guides in live scenes; the palimpsest keeper whose identity is their layers over time; or the "enough skins" conservative — every archetype in the skin is, by construction, an insider who wants the project to continue, so the §3 case for stopping has no embodied holder.

**Thin topics.** No topic is single-pole: every topic carries 2–3 poles. Two topics are xy-switchboxes (The cross slider, Shared appetite), whose poles are crossed independent yes/no axes in the widget's form, though the skin's own hovertips argue them as alternatives; §3 argues each pole on its own terms. Several Shaping-it topics (Eastern stacks, The civilizational octad) carry an abstention pole (Not my question / I haven't followed it) — a rated refusal to rule, which is a real position in this skin's governance sense but gives the indaba no substantive backing to work with beyond the ethics of abstention itself, argued in §3.

**Skin-JSON problems (reported, not fixed).** (1) All 32 topics have an empty `question` field — the whole skin states its questions only in hovertips. In each case the hovertip's `issue` states the question in full: §2 quotes it at the topic as the recovered question, and §5.1 collects all thirty-two verbatim. (2) The skin's `domain` field is the string "flex.capades.io" — a subdomain, not a domain description like other skins carry; §1 takes the domain framing from the skin's `about` instead, which states it fully. (3) Archetype emblem paths carry the full `flexcapades-` prefix (e.g. images/emblems/flexcapades-the-lawgiver.svg) rather than the shorter id-derived names other skins use; §4 lists them as the JSON gives them. No other problems found: every archetype rates all 32 topics (count 32 each).

**Deliberately left out of this research.** Any usage statistics for the app itself — none are published, and §3 says so at every pole rather than estimating; internal project deliberations beyond the open questions the skin itself lists; and any statistic not listed in §6 — there are none, by the same rule.
