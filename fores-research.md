## 1. Domain framing

Forescapades is an interactive hypermap for rating and visualizing what you believe about God, scripture, and the life of faith — and comparing your convictions with another person's, belief by belief, on the same picture. Build a personal profile, see where your faiths meet and where they part, use Scribe to write out what you hold true, and Scene Setter to plan a conversation about what matters most — all in the same shared vocabulary. The domain is The Abrahamic Faiths — Judaism, Christianity, and Islam. A position in this skin is a rated stance on one of its 118 topics: for each pole a holder advocates for it, against it, or toward it with aversion (negative advocacy is aversion, not absence of a view). Holding no stance is not a position; a 0 rating is. The meter word is **belief**: the skin measures how strongly a profile stands for each pole (advocacy, per pole) and how open it is to each topic at all (openness, per topic). Advocacy per pole and openness per topic are the inputs. Receptivity and intensity are derived, never rated: receptivity = round-half-even(openness × advocacy), computed with exact fractions, and intensity = the signed largest-|value| pole. The Scribe is an indaba / consensus process: it uses this research to show each position with its backing, name where holders meet and where they part, and ask what would let the group reach consensus. The skin's stance is the one stated in its own `about` text above: it maps the domain's live positions in one shared vocabulary, with no pole treated as the default.

## 5. Gap analysis

**Positions with no archetype.** The skin's 343 poles are far wider than its 58 archetypes, so most individual poles have no archetype that primarily embodies them; the archetypes embody families of positions, and §3 carries the backing for poles no single archetype centres.

**Thin topics.** 8 topic(s) carry a single pole — Knowledge of God and revelation, Providence and evil, Sharia (Islamic law), Discipline and membership, Christmas and Easter observance, Euthanasia and assisted dying, Oaths and swearing, Heaven and the new creation — so they register affinity but no disagreement; as single-pole affinity topics they are valid (negative ratings encode aversion), but they are thin for an indaba because there is no rival pole to locate a meeting point against. What would fill them: a genuine rival pole, added to the skin itself, not invented here.

**Switchbox topics.** 11 topic(s) use the xy-switchbox widget — Transcendence vs. Immanence, One God or many gods, Tradition vs. Reform, Priesthood and race, Boundaries of the Fold, Creed vs. Deed, The Eucharist, Eternal marriage, Blood transfusions, Exaltation, Who goes to heaven — whose two poles are two crossed independent yes/no axes by design, not opposite poles; the status quo on such a topic is both poles negative. They are not flagged as errors.

**Skin-JSON problems (reported, not fixed).** 20 topic(s) have an empty `question` field — Transcendence vs. Immanence (xfo0094), Mysticism vs. Rationalism (xfo0100), One God or many gods (xfo0108), Problem of Evil (xfo0097), Pre-existence of souls (xfo0112), Human origins (xfo0116), Tradition vs. Reform (xfo0093), Clerical celibacy (xfo0114), Priesthood and race (xfo0118), Boundaries of the Fold (xfo0098), Religion & State (xfo0092), Creed vs. Deed (xfo0096), Entire sanctification (xfo0117), Scope of Salvation (xfo0095), Mary: conception and assumption (xfo0113), Eternal marriage (xfo0111), Blood transfusions (xfo0109), Asceticism vs. Sanctified Joy (xfo0099), Exaltation (xfo0110), Who goes to heaven (xfo0115) — while carrying a hovertip issue; §2 and §3 above use that hovertip issue as the de facto question, as instructed.
