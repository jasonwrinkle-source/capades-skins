# Lingua — Research

Skin: Linguacapades (`linguacapades.json`) · Domain: language — rules, dialect, thought, the world's tongues, script, change and learning · Meter: sensibility
Coverage: 40 topics, 90 poles, 5 archetypes (all counts taken from the skin JSON).

## 1. Domain framing

Lingua maps language itself: who sets its rules, what a dialect says about its speaker, whether words shape thought, what is happening to the world's tongues, and how languages are written, changed, learned and mechanised. In the skin's own words: it maps where you stand on language — positions everyone holds and almost no one has ever stated, until they compare maps. The meter word is **sensibility**: the skin measures the cast of a profile's feeling for language — what it finds correct, beautiful, endangered or alive — expressed as rated stances. A position here is one pole of one topic, advocated for or against: positive advocacy is standing for the pole, negative advocacy is aversion to it, and a 0 rating (no stake) is also a recorded position. What does not count as a position is a mere fact about a language (how many speakers it has, how its verbs work) with no stance taken on it. The rating contract: the inputs are advocacy per pole and openness per topic; receptivity is derived as round-half-even(openness × advocacy) computed with exact fractions, never rated directly, and intensity is the signed largest-|value| pole, likewise derived, never rated. Multi-pole poles are rated independently and need not be opposites; the one xy-switchbox topic (Creoles) crosses two independent yes/no axes by design, whose status quo is both axes negative. The Scribe is an indaba: it uses this research to show each position with its backing, name where holders meet and part, and ask what would let the group reach consensus. The skin's stance is pluralist and descriptive-leaning but not neutral-by-omission: every camp — academy, vernacular, revivalist, engineer — is stated in its own strongest terms, and no pole is treated as the default.

**Positions with no archetype.** With only five archetypes for 90 poles, most poles have no archetype embodying them primarily: no archetype is chiefly the phonemic spelling reformer, the translanguaging advocate, the foreignizing translator, the emoji-as-paralanguage theorist, or the machine-translation optimist — those positions exist on the map and in §3 but must be argued by profiles, not personified. The five archetypes cluster on the rules/endangerment axis (ecologist, stylist, pragmatist, nativist, utilitarian), leaving the Writing & script and Words in motion categories with no native archetype at all.

**Thin topics.** Scripts & identity carries a single pole (Script is identity) — a valid affinity position whose negative is aversion, noted here as thin, not as an error: the indaba can list its backing (§3) but cannot locate a meeting point on it. Creoles (xy-switchbox) is two crossed axes by design, not a pair of opposites, and is not thin in the same sense. Several two-pole topics pair positions that are not strict opposites (Animal & AI language crosses an animal-continuity axis with an LLM-understanding axis), so movement on one pole does not imply movement on the other.

**Skin-JSON problems (reported, not fixed).** (1) Every one of the 40 topics has an empty `question` field; in each case the hovertip's `issue` is the de facto question, and §2 quotes it as such. (2) The top-level `domain` field holds a hostname (`lingua.capades.io`) rather than a domain description; §1 takes the framing from the skin's `about` text instead. (3) On several topics (Prescriptivism, Spelling reform, Animal & AI language, among others) hovertip pole `text` values are two-element lists repeating the label before the sentence, where other topics use plain strings — §2 and §3 use the sentence element. (4) One topic's `archetype` field points to a value (`simplex`) that is a rating-shape marker rather than one of the five archetype sample ids, on several topics — harmless for research, noted for the bridge.

**Deliberately left out of this research.** Language-by-language description (the skin maps stances about language, not grammars of particular tongues); partisan positions on specific national language laws except where a pole is explicitly about them; and any statistic not listed in §6 — where §3 could not ground a number in a source named with confidence, it says so rather than estimating.
