# Quanta — Research

Skin: Quantacapades (`quantacapades.json`) · Domain: Theoretical Physics — the pen-and-paper, blackboard, and chalk world where mathematical structure attempts to unveil the foundational architecture of physical reality · Meter: understanding
Coverage: 70 topics, 221 poles, 15 archetypes (all counts taken from the skin JSON).

## 1. Domain framing

Quanta maps theoretical physics: what the quantum state is, whether spacetime is fundamental, how the universe began and what it is made of, where macroscopic law comes from, and how a theorist should work. A position here is a rated stance on one of the skin's 70 questions — a pole a holder advocates for, against, or toward which they are averse (negative advocacy is aversion, not absence). Holding no stance is not a position; a 0 rating is. The meter word is **understanding**: the skin measures how strongly a profile stands for each pole (advocacy, per pole) and how open it is to each topic at all (openness, per topic). Receptivity and intensity are not rated directly — receptivity is derived as round-half-even(openness × advocacy) computed with exact fractions, and intensity is the signed largest-|value| pole, likewise derived. The Scribe is an indaba: it uses this research to show each position with its backing, name where holders meet and part, and ask what would let the group reach consensus. The skin's stance is pluralist and foundational: rival interpretations, rival quantum-gravity programmes, and rival working styles are placed on one map in one vocabulary, with no programme treated as the default and testability treated as a virtue a position may claim but not a gate a position must pass to appear.

## 2. Topic rationale

### Foundations & Interpretation

**Quantum Interpretations** — The master choice beneath the whole category: what the quantum state is. Copenhagen / Epistemic treats the wavefunction as knowledge and probabilities, with collapse on measurement; Many-Worlds / Everettian keeps unitary evolution absolute and lets the universe branch; De Broglie-Bohm / Pilot Wave restores definite particle trajectories guided by a real wave; QBism makes the state one agent's Bayesian credences; GRW / Objective Collapse makes collapse a real spontaneous physical process; Relational Quantum Mechanics makes states relative to interacting systems.

**Wavefunction** — The ontology question in its purest line form. ψ-Ontic says the wavefunction is real physical structure; Relational & Perspectival State says it exists only relative to an interacting system; ψ-Epistemic says it encodes an agent's information or beliefs.

**Measurement** — What, if anything, happens at measurement. Collapse says the wavefunction physically collapses, in objective versions via gravity or non-linear terms; Decoherence says entanglement with the environment creates the appearance of collapse without any break in unitary evolution; No-Collapse says the problem dissolves — all branches are real, or the problem was a pseudo-problem.

**Determinism** — Whether chance is in the world or in our description. Strict Determinism holds that the total state evolves deterministically and probability is coarse-graining or branch perspective; Mostly deterministic allows irreducible chance at the seams; Irreducible Randomness holds that quantum events are uncaused objective dice-rolls.

**Bell's theorem** — The forced choice Bell's inequality imposes. Non-Local Realism keeps definite trajectories and pays with Einsteinian locality; Superdeterminism / Retrocausality gives up measurement independence instead; Local Anti-Realism keeps relativistic locality and pays with counterfactual definiteness; Local branching derives the correlations from branching, with every outcome happening somewhere.

**What counts as an observer?** — Recovered question (skin's `question` field is empty; taken verbatim from the topic's hovertip issue): “What counts as an observer — a classical apparatus, any physical system, a conscious agent, or nobody at all?” Classical apparatus makes a classically described device the thing that collapses the state; Any physical system lets any interacting system be the relatum of a fact; The agent makes the observer the agent whose beliefs the state encodes; No privileged observer denies collapse and any special observer role, leaving branching to explain single outcomes.

**Where does quantum probability come from?** — Recovered question (skin's `question` field is empty; taken verbatim from the topic's hovertip issue): “Is quantum probability a law of nature, a fact about branching, a measure of belief, or a collapse rate?” Fundamental (Born rule) treats the Born rule as basic and irreducible; Emergent (branching) treats probability as a weight over branches; Subjective (belief) treats the numbers as an agent's degrees of belief; Objective (collapse) treats probability as the objective rate of spontaneous collapse.

**Can your interpretation be tested?** — Recovered question (skin's `question` field is empty; taken verbatim from the topic's hovertip issue): “Can we ever test which interpretation is right, or is the choice between them a matter of taste?” Yes, testable says the interpretation predicts something standard theory does not; No, not in principle says rival interpretations match the same experiments forever; The question is misposed says an interpretation is a framework for using a theory, not a rival theory awaiting a test.

**"Shut Up and Calculate!" vs. Foundational Realism** — The working-philosopher's fork: compute, or resolve ontology first. Operational Pragmatism First puts computing observables ahead; Foundations Only When Testable permits foundational work when it can cash out empirically; Foundational Clarity Is Primary holds that unresolved ontology is itself the obstacle.

**Does Decoherence Solve the Measurement Problem?** — Whether environment-induced decoherence finishes the measurement problem or only its easiest part. **Wigner's Friend & Observer-Independence of Facts** — Whether measurement facts are absolute. **Reconstructing Quantum Theory from Operational Axioms** — Whether quantum mechanics can be derived, not just postulated. Hilbert Space Is Primitive Physical Law treats the formalism as bedrock; Illuminating Consistency Check values reconstructions as checks, not explanations; Operational Information Axioms Explain "Why QM?" holds that information-theoretic principles explain why nature is quantum.

**Indefinite Causal Order & Quantum Switches** — Whether "A before B" can itself be superposed. **Role of Paradoxes & Thought Experiments** — What thought experiments are for. ## 3. Reasons and statistics backing each position

### 3.1 Foundations & Interpretation

#### Quantum Interpretations
- **Copenhagen / Epistemic (Orthodox)** — Reasons: the wavefunction predicts every experiment ever performed, so adding unobserved machinery is surplus ontology; complementarity dissolves pseudo-questions by tying descriptions to experimental arrangements. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an experiment whose outcome Copenhagen rules cannot state without the extra machinery a rival supplies.
- **Many-Worlds / Everettian** — Reasons: unitary evolution is the one dynamical law quantum mechanics actually states, and collapse is an extra postulate added by hand; taking the Schrödinger equation literally removes the measurement problem rather than solving it by fiat. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a confirmed deviation from unitary evolution, such as an observed objective collapse.
- **De Broglie-Bohm / Pilot Wave** — Reasons: particles with definite positions at all times restore a clear ontology in which measurement is ordinary interaction; determinism is recovered, with randomness only from ignorance of initial positions distributed by the Born rule. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a relativistic or field-theoretic failure of the pilot-wave programme that a rival ontology survives.
- **QBism / Agent-Centred Bayesianism** — Reasons: probabilities everywhere else in science are an agent's degrees of belief, and quantum states behave exactly like such beliefs, updating on experience; treating the state as personal dissolves Wigner's-friend contradictions without new physics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a theorem showing quantum probabilities must be objective — for instance a derivation of the Born rule that no belief-based reading can reproduce.
- **GRW / Objective Collapse** — Reasons: macroscopic superpositions are never seen, and a real stochastic collapse explains that fact dynamically instead of by observer decree; uniquely among interpretations, it makes new predictions, so it can be wrong — and therefore can be physics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: interferometry or optomechanics excluding the remaining GRW parameter space.
- **Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM)** — Reasons: relativity already taught that simultaneity is relative, and RQM extends the lesson — facts are relative to interacting systems, with no view from nowhere required; any system can play the observer role, so no special classical realm or consciousness is needed. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a no-go result showing relational facts cannot be consistently combined into the shared world experimenters actually report.

#### Wavefunction
- **ψ-Ontic** — Reasons: the Pusey–Barrett–Rudolph theorem shows that, given preparation independence, distinct quantum states correspond to distinct physical states, so the wavefunction cannot be mere information; interference and entanglement behave like features of a real field, not of anyone's knowledge. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a ψ-epistemic model reproducing all quantum predictions without the theorem's assumptions doing hidden ontic work.
- **Relational & Perspectival State** — Reasons: different observers legitimately assign different states to the same system, as Wigner's friend shows, and a state that is only relative explains this without contradiction; physics has repeatedly relativized absolutes — simultaneity, rest — and the quantum state is next. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an experiment demonstrating an observer-independent fact where relational accounts require relativity.
- **ψ-Epistemic** — Reasons: collapse looks exactly like Bayesian updating of information, abrupt and observer-dependent in a way no physical wave's collapse naturally is; epistemic readings explain no-cloning and teleportation as facts about information, not matter. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: acceptance that the PBR theorem's assumptions are unavoidable, forcing the state into the ontology.

#### Measurement
- **Collapse** — Reasons: measurements have single outcomes, and only a physical collapse delivers one from a superposition without multiplying worlds or observers; objective collapse versions are testable, converting the measurement problem from philosophy into experiment. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: matter-wave interference at masses where collapse models require the superposition to have already died.
- **Decoherence** — Reasons: environment-induced decoherence demonstrably selects pointer states and suppresses interference on calculable timescales, which is the part of the measurement problem experiments actually probe; no new dynamics is needed if apparent collapse is what unitary entanglement with an environment looks like from inside. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a derivation gap that proves decoherence cannot, even in principle, account for the definiteness of outcomes without an added postulate the holder accepts.
- **No-Collapse** — Reasons: nothing in the formalism ever signals a collapse, so positing one multiplies mechanisms beyond necessity; branching or dissolution keeps one dynamics for micro and macro alike. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: any verified non-unitary event — a single confirmed collapse would end the position.

#### Determinism
- **Strict Determinism** — Reasons: the Schrödinger equation is deterministic, and apparent randomness enters only through initial-condition ignorance (Bohm) or branch-relative perspective (Everett); a deterministic cosmos preserves the explanatory ideal that every event has a sufficient reason. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: proof that no deterministic completion can reproduce quantum statistics without conspiratorial initial conditions.
- **Mostly deterministic** — Reasons: deterministic law governs almost everything we engineer and predict, while collapse events, if real, are rare seams of chance; the position matches the evidence domain by domain instead of legislating for all of it at once. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a unified account — fully deterministic or fully indeterministic — that predicts the seams' location better than this hybrid does.
- **Irreducible Randomness** — Reasons: Bell violations exclude local hidden causes, and collapse theories make chance a law of nature rather than a confession of ignorance; quantum randomness is certified device-independently in modern Bell tests, which no deterministic story predicted. Statistic: the 2015 loophole-free Bell test at Delft reported a Bell parameter S = 2.42 ± 0.20, violating the local-realist bound of 2 (Hensen et al., Nature, 2015). What would move a holder: a deterministic model reproducing device-independent randomness certification without superdeterministic assumptions.

#### Bell's theorem
- **Non-Local Realism** — Reasons: keeping real particles with definite states is worth more than a locality that quantum correlations already falsify in spirit; Bohmian nonlocality is explicit and honest, and no signal can be sent with it, so relativity's letter survives. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a relativistic pilot-wave theory failing where a local account succeeds, or evidence that nonlocal guidance conflicts with an experiment.
- **Superdeterminism / Retrocausality** — Reasons: measurement independence is an assumption, not a law, and dropping it restores locality and realism together — the only option that keeps both; retrocausal models explain correlations by influences along the light cones, with no action at a distance. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that measurement settings can be certified independent of the measured system to a precision that closes the superdeterministic loophole.
- **Local Anti-Realism** — Reasons: "unperformed experiments have no results" is the cheapest exit — locality and no-conspiracy survive if counterfactual definiteness is surrendered; the view matches operational practice, in which only performed measurements enter the record. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a compelling account of what, if not pre-existing values, makes measurement outcomes stable and intersubjectively checkable.
- **Local branching** — Reasons: in an Everettian picture each measurement's outcomes occur in decohered branches, so correlations are explained by branch structure with no influence crossing space; locality is preserved at the level of the universal wavefunction's local Hamiltonians. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a confirmed collapse or nonlocal signal showing branching is not what measurement does.

#### What counts as an observer?
- **Classical apparatus** — Reasons: every actual experiment terminates in a macroscopic, classically describable record, and Bohr's point stands — results must be communicable in classical language; drawing the cut at the apparatus matches laboratory practice exactly. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a working measurement theory in which no classical/quantum cut appears anywhere, yet records remain unambiguous.
- **Any physical system** — Reasons: nothing in physics marks out devices or minds as special, and interactions are ubiquitous — any system a system interacts with can anchor a fact relative to it; this democratizes the observer and removes the dualism of a classical realm. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that only systems above some complexity or classicality threshold ever produce stable records.
- **The agent** — Reasons: quantum states are used by agents to bet on their future experiences, and locating the observer in the agent makes collapse a belief update with no physical mystery; the view predicts exactly the observer-dependence Wigner's-friend scenarios display. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a case where an agent's state assignment is forced by the world rather than chosen by the agent's information.
- **No privileged observer** — Reasons: if unitary evolution never breaks, no observer needs privileging — branching does all the work observers were invented for; privileging observers has historically been where dualisms hide. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a verified observer-dependent physical effect that branching cannot reproduce.

#### Where does quantum probability come from?
- **Fundamental (Born rule)** — Reasons: every theory has primitives, and the Born rule's probabilities have survived a century of attempts to derive or replace them; treating probability as basic matches collapse theories, in which nature herself rolls the dice. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a derivation of the Born rule from premises the holder finds weaker than the rule itself.
- **Emergent (branching)** — Reasons: if every outcome occurs, probability must be a measure over branches, and decision-theoretic derivations show rational agents in a branching universe must bet by Born weights; this explains probability instead of postulating it. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a proof that branch weights cannot ground rational credence — that the decision-theoretic derivation is circular beyond repair.
- **Subjective (belief)** — Reasons: probabilities in quantum state assignments change with information alone, which is the signature of belief, not of worldly chance; Bayesian coherence, not physics, is what constrains the numbers an agent may hold. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a physical process whose rates fix probabilities independently of any agent's information, as objective-collapse advocates claim.
- **Objective (collapse)** — Reasons: GRW-style dynamics assigns collapse a definite stochastic rate per particle per second, making probability as objective as radioactive decay; the objectivity is testable, because the rate predicts measurable heating and interference loss. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: experimental exclusion of the collapse rates this objectivity requires.

#### Can your interpretation be tested?
- **Yes, testable** — Reasons: objective collapse theories already deviate from standard quantum mechanics at large mass and long coherence, so interpretation choice is partly an experimental question; insisting interpretations are untestable freezes a contingent fact about today's instruments into a principle. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a proof that every empirically distinct interpretation collapses, on inspection, into a notational variant of the others.
- **No, not in principle** — Reasons: the major interpretations were constructed to reproduce the same predictions, and a century of ingenuity has produced no experiment separating the leading ones; intellectual honesty means admitting the choice currently rests on explanatory virtues, not data. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: one confirmed prediction by any interpretation that its rivals cannot match.
- **The question is misposed** — Reasons: an interpretation is a framework for using and understanding a theory, as a coordinate system is for geometry — frameworks are judged by fruitfulness, not falsified; asking for the crucial experiment mistakes a hermeneutic choice for a hypothesis. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an interpretation generating a novel confirmed prediction, showing it was a rival theory all along.

#### "Shut Up and Calculate!" vs. Foundational Realism
- **Operational Pragmatism First** — Reasons: calculational quantum mechanics built transistors, lasers and MRI while foundational argument produced no device; a field is judged by the observables it delivers, and foundational puzzles have historically dissolved under better calculation, not better metaphysics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a foundational insight that directly enables a technology or prediction calculation alone had missed — as Bell's theorem enabled device-independent cryptography.
- **Foundations Only When Testable** — Reasons: foundational work earns its keep when it cashes out — Bell turned metaphysics into an experiment, and that is the model; rationing foundational effort by testability keeps the field honest without banning the questions. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: repeated cases where untestable foundational work later proved indispensable, weakening the testability ration.
- **Foundational Clarity Is Primary** — Reasons: a theory nobody can state without contradiction is unfinished physics, however well it computes — Einstein's objections, not calculation, forced quantum theory's deepest clarifications; ambiguity about what the theory says propagates into quantum gravity, where calculation alone cannot arbitrate. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that every foundational puzzle dissolves under decoherence and careful calculation, leaving nothing for ontology to do.

#### Does Decoherence Solve the Measurement Problem?
- **Solves It For All Practical Purposes** — Reasons: decoherence explains preferred bases, suppression of interference, and the emergence of classical records with quantitative, experimentally confirmed timescales; demanding more than FAPP definiteness asks physics to answer a question no experiment can pose. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an experiment displaying interference where decoherence theory says the record should already be classical and single.
- **Explains Basis, Not Definite Outcomes** — Reasons: decoherence turns a pure state into an improper mixture, and an improper mixture is not one outcome — the "and" does not become "or" by entanglement with air molecules; crediting decoherence with its real achievement (the basis) while denying the overclaim keeps both halves of the truth. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a rigorous argument that improper mixtures suffice for definite outcomes, accepted across the interpretations this holder respects.
- **Leaves Core Problem Untouched** — Reasons: the measurement problem is the problem of single outcomes, and decoherence presupposes the Born rule and the system/apparatus split it was meant to explain; a century after von Neumann the chain still ends in an unanalysed "measurement." Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a decoherence-based derivation of single outcomes and the Born rule that does not smuggle them in as premises.

#### Wigner's Friend & Observer-Independence of Facts
- **Facts Are Strictly Observer-Relative** — Reasons: extended Wigner's-friend arguments show that universal quantum theory, single outcomes, and observer-independent facts cannot all hold, and relativity of facts is the premise cheapest to keep; experiments violating "local friendliness" inequalities point the same way. Statistic: a photonic experiment violated local-friendliness inequalities, testing the assumptions behind observer-independent facts (Bong et al., Nature Physics, 2020). What would move a holder: a formulation of quantum theory preserving absolute facts that reproduces the friend-scenario statistics without hidden observer-dependence.
- **Contextual Yet Inter-Subjective** — Reasons: facts arise in contexts — interactions — but contexts can be compared and cross-checked, which is all the objectivity science has ever used; the position keeps Wigner's-friend lessons without dissolving the shared world laboratories report. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstrated case of two contexts whose records cannot be consistently cross-checked in principle.
- **Single Objective Reality of Facts** — Reasons: science rests on records that all observers can consult, and a physics in which facts themselves fragment threatens the evidential base of every other claim in this skin; collapse and Bohmian theories show objective facts are available at acceptable prices. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: acceptance that the friend-scenario no-go theorems leave no objective-facts formulation standing.

#### Reconstructing Quantum Theory from Operational Axioms
- **Hilbert Space Is Primitive Physical Law** — Reasons: reconstructions derive the formalism from axioms chosen because they yield it, which risks circularity dressed as explanation; Hilbert space has earned primitive status the way spacetime has — by a century of unmatched service. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a reconstruction whose axioms are independently motivated by information practice and that predicts a deviation or a neighbouring theory nature actually uses.
- **Illuminating Consistency Check** — Reasons: derivations from purification, local tomography and continuous reversibility show quantum theory is not an arbitrary point in theory space, which disciplines speculation about its replacements; as checks they also map exactly where a post-quantum theory would have to depart. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a reconstruction that yields a confirmed novel prediction, promoting it from check to explanation in the holder's eyes.
- **Operational Information Axioms Explain "Why QM?"** — Reasons: that nature's theory is the unique one satisfying simple information principles — no cloning, purification, continuous reversibility — explains its form the way the light postulate explains Lorentz transformations; the derivations unify quantum theory with information practice instead of leaving Hilbert space a brute gift. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: proof that the favoured axioms covertly assume the Hilbert-space structure they claim to derive.

#### Indefinite Causal Order & Quantum Switches
- **Fixed Background Spacetime Causality** — Reasons: every quantum-switch experiment to date runs on an ordinary spacetime with definite order at the level of the laboratory, so the indefiniteness is in the control qubit's description, not in causation; a fixed causal background is what general relativity and all confirmed physics actually use. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a gravitational quantum switch — superposed spacetime itself — showing causal order indefinite in the geometry, not only in a circuit.
- **Useful Operational Quantum Circuit Tool** — Reasons: the quantum switch demonstrably yields advantages in communication complexity and channel discrimination, whatever its metaphysics; operational resources do not need a settled ontology to be worth developing. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either a no-go result capping the switch's advantages, or a gravitational realization promoting it from tool to fundamental feature.
- **Fundamental Quantum Gravity Feature** — Reasons: a quantum spacetime cannot assign every pair of events a definite order, and process matrices are the first formalism that takes that seriously; the switch is to quantum gravity what superposition was to quantum mechanics — the small, exact model of the big idea. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a mature quantum gravity theory in which causal order turns out definite after all.

#### Role of Paradoxes & Thought Experiments
- **Wait for Experimental Anomalies** — Reasons: physics' revolutions that lasted — relativity's, quantum theory's — were forced by data (Michelson–Morley, blackbody spectra), while pure paradox-mongering has produced decades of firewall-style stalemate; anomalies are nature's objections, which outrank ours. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a paradox-driven programme delivering a confirmed prediction before any anomaly demanded it.
- **Targeted Conceptual Checks** — Reasons: paradoxes are cheap stress-tests that locate hidden assumptions — EPR found entanglement's significance decades before experiments could; used as diagnostics rather than manifestos, they cost little and have repeatedly paid. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a track record showing paradox-checking systematically misallocates effort compared with anomaly-driven work.
- **Paradoxes Drive Physics Revolutions** — Reasons: EPR, the black-hole information paradox and Wigner's friend each restructured a field's questions, and the information paradox alone generated holography, islands and the modern gravity programme; a theory's contradictions with itself are data about the theory, available now, without waiting for instruments. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the major live paradoxes dissolving under existing theory with no conceptual residue, showing the engine has run out of fuel.

### 3.2 Quantum Gravity & Spacetime

#### The Quantum Gravity Crossroads
- **String / M-Theory** — Reasons: string theory is the only programme that has produced a perturbatively finite quantum gravity, a microscopic count of black-hole entropy, and a working holography; its dualities keep revealing that apparently different theories are one structure, the historical signature of being onto something. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a rival programme matching string theory's black-hole entropy counts and holographic results from fewer assumptions.
- **Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG)** — Reasons: LQG quantizes Einstein's theory itself, honouring background independence instead of quantizing fluctuations on a stage Einstein abolished; area and volume come out discrete from the theory, a prediction-shaped result no rival handed us first. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that the semiclassical limit cannot recover smooth spacetime and low-energy gravity as observed.
- **Asymptotic Safety (FRG)** — Reasons: a non-Gaussian UV fixed point would make quantum gravity predictive with finitely many parameters, using ordinary quantum field theory's own tools; functional renormalization evidence for the fixed point has accumulated across truncations for two decades. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: truncations converging on the fixed point's disappearance, or a proof that the fixed point cannot support the matter content of the Standard Model.
- **Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT)** — Reasons: CDT's Lorentzian path integral over triangulations generates an extended four-dimensional de Sitter-like spacetime dynamically, where Euclidean approaches produced only crumpled or branched-polymer phases; it is a minimal, computable route from quantum geometry to a classical-looking cosmos. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that CDT's emergent geometry fails to reproduce classical gravity at large scales, or that its continuum limit does not exist.
- **Causal Sets & Discrete Approaches** — Reasons: order plus number equals geometry — a causal set's partial order and counting recover Lorentzian geometry, and discreteness is forced to be Lorentz-invariant in a way lattices are not; the programme even produced a heuristic estimate of the cosmological constant's order of magnitude before its measurement era. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a no-go result showing causal-set dynamics cannot recover Einstein's equations with the observed matter content.
- **Emergent / Thermodynamic Gravity** — Reasons: Einstein's equations can be derived from thermodynamics of local horizons, suggesting gravity is an equation of state, not a fundamental interaction to quantize; quantizing an emergent phenomenon is the historical error of quantizing sound to find atoms of phonons — the atoms are underneath, not in the wave equation. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a tabletop demonstration that gravity entangles quantum masses, showing gravity itself carries quantum degrees of freedom.

#### Spacetime
- **Fundamental Spacetime** — Reasons: every confirmed theory presupposes spacetime, and emergence claims have yet to derive the full Einstein equations with matter from anything more basic in our universe; the continuum has survived every probe, from accelerators to gravitational waves, without a hint of granularity. Statistic: gravitational-wave event GW170817 and its gamma-ray counterpart constrained the difference between the speed of gravity and light to within about one part in 10^15 (Abbott et al., Physical Review Letters, 2017). What would move a holder: a derivation of Einstein gravity from entanglement or discrete structure that also predicts a confirmed deviation.
- **Quantum-Geometric Granularity** — Reasons: quantizing geometry in LQG yields discrete area and volume spectra, and causal sets and triangulations independently point to atomic spacetime; singularity theorems signal the continuum's breakdown exactly where granularity would replace it. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: precision tests excluding discreteness at scales where the granularity programme requires it.
- **Emergent Spacetime** — Reasons: in AdS/CFT, bulk geometry is demonstrably built from boundary entanglement — spatial connectivity tracks mutual information, and geometry responds to entanglement as to a substance; black-hole thermodynamics assigns entropy to area, not volume, as if geometry were a bookkeeping of information. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a proof that entanglement-based reconstruction fails outside anti-de Sitter toys in ways no extension repairs.

#### Supersymmetry (SUSY) Stance
- **Beautiful Failed Hypothesis for Nature** — Reasons: the LHC was built substantially to find weak-scale superpartners and found none across an enormous search programme; a symmetry nature declines to use at any accessible scale has the same status as any other falsified elegance, however lovely the mathematics. Statistic: ATLAS searches with 139 fb⁻¹ of 13 TeV data excluded gluinos up to roughly 2.2 TeV in simplified models (ATLAS Collaboration, 2020). What would move a holder: discovery of any superpartner, at any scale, in collider or precision data.
- **Realized Only at High / Planck Scales** — Reasons: split and high-scale SUSY keep gauge-coupling unification and dark-matter candidates while accepting the LHC's null results as a measurement of the breaking scale; absence at 1 TeV refutes one breaking scenario, not the symmetry. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a proof that high-scale breaking reintroduces the fine-tuning SUSY was invented to remove, in the holder's own accounting.
- **Still Essential Symmetry of Physics** — Reasons: supersymmetry remains the only known loophole in the Coleman–Mandula theorem, the unique way to extend spacetime symmetry itself; string theory, the leading quantum-gravity candidate, requires it, and the hierarchy problem it solves has not gone away. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a complete quantum gravity and particle theory that solves the hierarchy problem and unifies couplings without supersymmetry anywhere.

#### Singularities
- **Quantum Bounce / Invariant Cutoff** — Reasons: loop quantum cosmology replaces the Big Bang singularity with a bounce at a universal maximum density, derived from the quantum geometry rather than inserted; a bounce preserves determinism through what classical theory calls the beginning. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: observational exclusion of bounce signatures in the cosmic microwave background, or failure of the bounce under generic (non-symmetric) collapse.
- **Finite density** — Reasons: several independent approaches — asymptotic safety, string gas, limiting-curvature constructions — cap density at a finite Planck-scale value; a finite cap is the minimal modification that removes infinities without committing to one programme's full machinery. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a theorem that finite-density resolutions generically violate energy conditions in ways observations exclude.
- **Smooth Quantum Beginning (No-Boundary / Tunneling)** — Reasons: the Hartle–Hawking and Vilenkin proposals give the universe a well-defined quantum origin with no initial singularity and no prior state to explain; the beginning becomes a boundary condition in the path integral, mathematically on par with any other. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that these proposals' probability measures decisively favour universes unlike ours.
- **Spacetime Melts into a Pre-Geometric Phase** — Reasons: multiple programmes (CDT's phases, string theory's high-energy regime, group field theory condensates) exhibit phases in which geometry itself is absent; a phase transition explains why "before spacetime" is a category error rather than a mystery. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a singularity resolution entirely within geometry, making the pre-geometric phase unnecessary.
- **Physical Singularity & Spacetime Breakdown** — Reasons: the singularity theorems are theorems — given their energy and causality assumptions, geodesic incompleteness follows, and wishing it away by quantization is a hope, not a result; an honest breakdown may be the true message: spacetime ends, and physics must say so. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: any complete, singularity-free quantum gravity that reproduces classical collapse in its domain.

#### Theory scope
- **GUT** — Reasons: the three gauge couplings nearly meet at high energy, and their near-miss under Standard Model running becomes a hit under plausible extensions — a quantitative hint no rival explains; charge quantization falls out of grand unification for free. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: proton-decay limits and precision coupling measurements jointly excluding the unification window for all viable groups.
- **EFT now, GUT as horizon** — Reasons: effective field theory is the tool that actually delivers predictions at every accessible scale, while unification remains the best-motivated direction for the next step; holding the horizon without pretending to stand on it is how the field in fact progressed from Fermi theory to the Standard Model. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either a confirmed unification signal (promoting the horizon to a programme) or a proof that the couplings' near-meeting is coincidence.
- **EFT** — Reasons: every historical "final" framework was an effective theory of the next scale down, and decoupling theorems explain why scale-by-scale physics works so well; unification has promised for fifty years and delivered no confirmed prediction, while EFT delivers daily. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a confirmed grand-unified prediction, such as proton decay at a GUT-predicted rate.

#### Ambition
- **Grand Unified Ambition (TOE)** — Reasons: the historical trend is unification — electricity with magnetism, both with the weak force, chemistry with physics — and stopping the trend by decree needs its own argument; a final theory is the only form in which "why these laws?" could receive a non-circular answer. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a principled impossibility result — a proof that no single framework can contain both quantum theory and gravity as we observe them.
- **Hedged ambition** — Reasons: the evidence cuts both ways — unification's track record against the landscape's suggestion that parameters may be environmental — and hedging is the calibrated response; a final theory may exist and still be unreachable by finite science, a distinction the confident on both sides blur. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either a working final theory (ending the hedge upward) or landscape evidence strong enough to end it downward.
- **Pragmatic Effective Field Theory (EFT)** — Reasons: Wilson taught that theories come with cutoffs and that physics is the art of the relevant scale; the TOE quest has consumed careers while EFT produced the Standard Model's precision era; humility about finality is a finding of twentieth-century physics, not a failure of nerve. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a candidate final theory making a confirmed, risky prediction.

#### Must Gravity Actually Be Quantized?
- **Gravity May Remain Classical** — Reasons: gravity is spacetime geometry, not a force in spacetime, and no experiment has ever shown a quantum property of gravity itself; semiclassical and hybrid models fit all data, and quantizing may be a category error driven by analogy with the other interactions. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: observation of gravity-mediated entanglement between quantum masses, which classical channels cannot generate.
- **Hybrid Classical–Quantum Dynamics** — Reasons: consistent stochastic hybrid models now exist that couple classical gravity to quantum matter without the pathologies Feynman assumed fatal; hybrids make distinctive predictions — anomalous heating and decoherence — so the option is testable, not evasive. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: hybrid models' predicted decoherence or heating being excluded at the levels the models require.
- **Gravity Must Be Fully Quantum** — Reasons: matter is quantum, gravity couples to matter, and every consistent coupling scheme tried either quantizes gravity or leaks inconsistencies; tabletop proposals to witness gravity-mediated entanglement exist precisely because the quantum character of gravity is an empirical question with an expected answer. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a fully consistent classical–quantum coupling surviving all proposed entanglement and consistency tests.

#### AdS/CFT & Holography Beyond Anti-de Sitter
- **Mathematical Toy in Wrong Spacetime** — Reasons: our universe has a positive cosmological constant and no timelike boundary, so AdS/CFT's precise machinery addresses a spacetime we do not inhabit; results proven in the toy are routinely over-imported into de Sitter reality on hope. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a controlled de Sitter holography importing AdS techniques with equal precision.
- **Deep Clue Needing dS Breakthrough** — Reasons: that gravity in any spacetime seems to be holographic — entropy on areas, information on boundaries — is too structural to be an artifact of negative Λ; the clue is real, and naming the missing de Sitter step honestly is the research programme. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either the dS breakthrough (promoting clue to principle) or a demonstration that holography depends essentially on the AdS boundary.
- **Universal Principle of Real Quantum Gravity** — Reasons: black-hole thermodynamics already taught, before any duality, that a region's information content scales with its boundary area — holography is in the data of gravity itself; AdS/CFT is the first precise instance of a principle every quantum gravity must obey. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a consistent quantum gravity provably violating holographic entropy bounds.

#### Background Independence Mandate
- **Strict Non-Perturbative Background Independence** — Reasons: Einstein's central lesson is that geometry is dynamical and no stage exists before the play; perturbative methods on fixed backgrounds build the lesson's negation into the foundations; a theory that presupposes a metric cannot explain the metric's origin or its quantum fluctuations non-perturbatively. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a background-dependent construction yielding a complete, singularity-free quantum gravity with background independence emerging exactly in the regimes where it is tested.

#### Quantum Complexity as Physical Geometry (Complexity = Volume / Action)
- **Arbitrary Gate-Set Metaphor** — Reasons: circuit complexity depends on an arbitrary choice of gate set and tolerance, and a physical geometry cannot depend on a theorist's conventions; the match of growth rates is one derivative agreeing, which is analogy, not identity. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a derivation fixing the gate-set ambiguity from gravity itself, making the identification convention-free.
- **Promising Semiclassical Clue** — Reasons: complexity grows linearly for exponential time, exactly as black-hole interiors grow long after thermal entropy saturates — the one bulk quantity that does; two independent conjectures (volume and action) bracketing the same growth is the shape clues take before they become theorems. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a bulk observable outgrowing complexity's linear bound, or a boundary computation breaking the match.
- **Circuit Complexity Drives Spacetime Growth** — Reasons: in holography the boundary theory is the reality and the bulk its representation, so if boundary complexity grows, the interior it encodes must grow — the direction of explanation runs from computation to geometry; the proposal unifies black-hole interiors with quantum information in one mechanism. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a holographic model in which interior volume grows while boundary complexity provably does not.

#### Celestial & Flat-Space Holography
- **Soft-Theorem Kinematical Reformulation** — Reasons: the celestial programme's established content — soft theorems as Ward identities, BMS symmetries, memory effects — is a repackaging of known infrared physics in conformal language; repackaging is valuable but should not be mistaken for a dual theory with its own dynamics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a celestial CFT computation of a scattering amplitude not previously obtainable from soft theorems and symmetry alone.
- **Valuable Stepping Stone to Real Spacetime** — Reasons: flat space is the spacetime of every scattering experiment ever performed, and a holography native to it would outrank AdS in relevance; even partial celestial results reorganize amplitudes productively on the way. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either completion of the celestial dictionary (promotion) or a structural obstruction showing the dictionary cannot close.
- **True Holographic Dual of Our Asymptotically Flat World** — Reasons: the infrared triangle — soft theorems, asymptotic symmetries, memory — closes exactly as a holographic dictionary should, with BMS symmetry acting as the dual's conformal symmetry; scattering states at null infinity are the boundary data, and celestial amplitudes their correlators. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: proof that celestial correlators cannot encode bulk locality or unitarity beyond the soft sector.

#### Cosmic Censorship Conjecture (Weak & Strong)
- **Nature Abhors Naked Singularities** — Reasons: censorship protects predictability itself — a naked singularity is a place where arbitrary data can enter the universe, ending law-governed physics; decades of attempts to manufacture one by over-spinning or over-charging black holes have failed by small, suggestive margins. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a physically realizable collapse scenario, within classical GR and reasonable matter, provably forming a naked singularity.
- **Holds Generically in 4D, Fails in Edge Cases** — Reasons: critical collapse and higher-dimensional pinch-offs show censorship's exceptions are real but measure-zero or outside four-dimensional astrophysics; a generic truth with classified exceptions is the normal form of a good physical principle. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an edge case shown to be generic under perturbation, or an astrophysical candidate requiring the exceptions.
- **Quantum Gravity Resolves Singularities Anyway** — Reasons: censorship is a conjecture inside a theory — classical GR — that its own singularity theorems show to be incomplete; debating the clothing of objects quantum gravity will dissolve spends effort on a question with a shelf life. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that some censorship question survives quantization with observable consequences.

#### Beyond General Relativity: Modified Gravity Landscape
- **Einstein's GR Is Uniquely Rigid** — Reasons: Lovelock's theorem shows Einstein's equations (with Λ) are the unique second-order, divergence-free, metric theory in four dimensions — modifications must pay with new fields, higher derivatives, or extra dimensions; a century of tests, now including gravitational-wave propagation, has found GR exact wherever it can be checked. Statistic: GW170817 bounded any difference between gravitational-wave speed and light speed to within about one part in 10^15, eliminating broad classes of modified gravity (Abbott et al., Physical Review Letters, 2017). What would move a holder: any confirmed deviation from GR — a fifth force, a dispersion in gravitational waves, a failed no-hair test.
- **GR + Higher-Derivative EFT Corrections** — Reasons: effective field theory guarantees higher-derivative corrections suppressed by a heavy scale, so GR as an EFT is not a rival to modification but its controlled form; this is the only modification class quantum considerations actually require. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an observed modification too large or too structured for EFT suppression to accommodate.
- **Scalar-Tensor, Massive & Torsion Extensions** — Reasons: cosmic acceleration and the cosmological constant problem are empirical facts GR accommodates only by unexplained constants, and new gravitational degrees of freedom remain live explanations; modified theories are also the indispensable foil — GR's tests mean little without alternatives to test against. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the surviving parameter space of these extensions being closed by observation while acceleration gains a non-gravitational explanation.

#### Strong-Field Gravitational-Wave Theory & Black-Hole Spectroscopy
- **Prioritizing Strong-Field Black-Hole Ringdown & No-Hair Tests** — Reasons: ringdown quasinormal modes are determined by mass and spin alone if Kerr and GR hold, so a spectrum is a fingerprint test of the most extreme regime physics can reach; numerical relativity has made the waveforms available, converting a theorem-test into an observational programme. Statistic: the first gravitational-wave detection, GW150914, observed a binary black-hole merger whose ringdown was consistent with a Kerr remnant (Abbott et al., Physical Review Letters, 2016). What would move a holder: ringdown measurements reaching precision where systematic waveform errors, not gravity, dominate every foreseeable test.

### 3.3 Cosmology & the Universe

#### Cosmic origin
- **Big Bang** — Reasons: the expanding universe, the cosmic microwave background, and light-element abundances converge on a hot dense early state with independent lines of evidence; no rival accounts for all three at once. Statistic: the Planck satellite measured the universe's age as 13.787 ± 0.020 billion years (Planck Collaboration, Astronomy & Astrophysics, 2020). What would move a holder: an observation — a CMB or abundance pattern — that a hot beginning cannot produce but a bounce or eternal cosmos predicts.
- **Cyclic** — Reasons: a bounce removes the beginning, and with it the question of what caused it; cyclic and ekpyrotic models make distinguishing predictions, notably in primordial gravitational waves, so the view risks itself empirically. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: detection of a primordial B-mode signal at the level inflation generically predicts and cyclic models generically exclude.
- **Steady State** — Reasons: the perfect cosmological principle — no special time as well as no special place — is the more Copernican cosmology, and its historical defeat taught the field how a beautiful principle dies by data; a remnant of holders prize it as the standing reminder that consensus cosmologies have been wrong before. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the position is already data-refuted in its classical form; only a new eternal cosmology reproducing CMB and abundance evidence wholesale could revive it.

#### Dark matter
- **Particle Dark Matter** — Reasons: gravitational evidence for dark matter appears on every scale from galaxies to the CMB acoustic peaks, and a particle explains all scales with one substance; structure formation requires matter that was already clustering before recombination, which baryons coupled to photons could not do. Statistic: Planck measured the cold-dark-matter density as Ω_c h² = 0.120 ± 0.001 (Planck Collaboration, Astronomy & Astrophysics, 2020). What would move a holder: a modified-gravity theory reproducing the CMB peaks, lensing and structure growth with no unseen mass.
- **Hybrid models** — Reasons: the evidence splits — cosmology behaves particle-like while galactic dynamics shows eerily law-like acceleration regularities — and a hybrid (for example superfluid or partially interacting dark matter) can honour both; insisting one mechanism explain every scale is a preference, not a finding. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either a direct particle detection (ending the need for the gravity half) or a galactic-scale derivation from pure particle physics (ending the need for the hybrid).
- **Modified Gravity / MOND** — Reasons: galactic rotation curves follow a single acceleration law with one constant, a₀, predicting curve shapes from baryons alone — a predictive success particle models achieve only galaxy by galaxy with tuned halos; where MOND has made advance predictions (low-surface-brightness galaxies), it has been right. Statistic: the radial acceleration relation was fit across 153 SPARC galaxies with a characteristic acceleration near 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² (McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert, Physical Review Letters, 2016). What would move a holder: a laboratory detection of a dark-matter particle with the right abundance, or a relativistic MOND theory failing a lensing test its particle rival passes.

#### Dark energy
- **Constant** — Reasons: the cosmological constant fits every dataset with one number and no dynamics, and two decades of increasingly precise surveys have found w consistent with −1; Λ is also what general relativity plus vacuum energy most plainly suggests, however uncomfortable its magnitude. Statistic: Planck combined data measured the dark-energy equation of state as w = −1.03 ± 0.03, consistent with a constant (Planck Collaboration, Astronomy & Astrophysics, 2020). What would move a holder: a statistically robust measurement of w differing from −1 or evolving with redshift.
- **Slowly dynamical** — Reasons: recent baryon-acoustic-oscillation results have hinted, at modest significance, at evolving dark energy, and prudence keeps the near-Λ dynamical window open; the position costs little — it agrees with Λ everywhere Λ has been tested. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: next-generation surveys (DESI's full sample, Euclid) either confirming evolution or squeezing it below any field's natural range.
- **Dynamical** — Reasons: a field explains why dark energy's density is comparable to matter's today only as a stage in an evolution, softening the coincidence problem; quintessence-style dynamics is generic in high-energy theory, whereas a bare constant of exactly the observed smallness is the anomaly demanding explanation. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: surveys pinning w to −1 with errors small enough that any rolling field would have to be frozen by fine-tuning worse than Λ's.

#### The Cosmological Constant Trilemma
- **Anthropic Landscape Selection** — Reasons: Weinberg's anthropic bound predicted a small nonzero Λ of roughly the observed order before its measurement-era confirmation, the one successful advance estimate in this problem; if a landscape of vacua exists, selection is not an excuse but the mechanism. Statistic: the string landscape's vacuum count is commonly estimated at around 10^500 (Bousso & Polchinski, 2000). What would move a holder: a symmetry or dynamical mechanism deriving Λ's value with no environmental input.
- **Undiscovered Symmetry** — Reasons: symmetries have repeatedly explained small numbers (chiral symmetry for pion masses, supersymmetry's cancellations), and Λ's smallness has the shape of a protected quantity; declaring the problem environmental before exhausting symmetry is surrender scheduled too early. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a no-go theorem excluding symmetry protection of vacuum energy in any consistent quantum gravity.
- **Modified Cosmic Gravity** — Reasons: Λ's effects appear only at cosmic acceleration scales, exactly where a modification of gravity in the infrared would show itself; changing the law rather than the inventory keeps the universe's contents to what is observed. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: gravitational-wave and large-scale-structure tests closing the infrared-modification window while ΛCDM keeps fitting.
- **Dynamical Screening / Dark Energy** — Reasons: screening and relaxation mechanisms make the observed Λ an output of dynamics — the vacuum's bare value can be large if it is screened or relaxed away; such mechanisms are in principle testable through fifth-force and time-variation searches. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: screening mechanisms being excluded across their viable parameter space by laboratory and astrophysical tests.
- **QFT Vacuum-Energy Calculation Is Misframed** — Reasons: Λ in Einstein's equations is a renormalized classical coupling, measured like any other, and the famous 10^120 mismatch compares it to a cutoff estimate no EFT practitioner would treat as a prediction; the "worst prediction in physics" is an artefact of asking flat-space bookkeeping to fix a gravitational coupling. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a UV-complete theory in which vacuum energy is calculable and comes out wrong without environmental or dynamical help.

#### Universes
- **Multiverse** — Reasons: eternal inflation, the string landscape and Everettian branching independently generate other universes, so the multiverse is a by-product of theories held for other reasons, not an invention to save Λ; testability objections apply unevenly — some multiverse models predict observable bubble-collision or curvature signatures. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the parent theories (inflation, string theory) being replaced by accounts with unique vacua and single outcomes.
- **Undecided** — Reasons: the evidence genuinely underdetermines the question — every argument runs through a contested parent theory; undecided is the calibrated stance when both affirmation and denial outrun the data. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: any of the parent theories reaching confirmation or refutation strong enough to carry its multiverse with it.
- **One Universe** — Reasons: a hypothesis that no observation could distinguish from its negation is not physics, and the multiverse's defenders have yet to produce the agreed measure that would make it predictive; parsimony awards the cosmos to the single universe until evidence, not consistency, demands more. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an observation — a bubble-collision signature, negative curvature from eternal inflation — that a single-universe cosmology cannot accommodate.

#### Arrow of time
- **Past Hypothesis (Low-Entropy Start)** — Reasons: every observed arrow — thermodynamic, psychological, radiative, cosmological — traces to the early universe's extraordinarily low entropy, one posit explaining all of them; statistical mechanics plus this boundary condition is a complete, working account needing no new dynamics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a derivation of the low-entropy start from dynamics the holder accepts, which would demote the posit to a theorem — or a domain where arrows point without it.
- **Gravitational / Dynamical Complexity** — Reasons: gravity's contribution to entropy is where the Past Hypothesis merely names the puzzle — a smooth early universe is gravitationally low-entropy, and complexity growth in gravitational systems gives the arrow a mechanism, not a posit; shape-dynamics versions derive directed time from structure formation itself. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a complexity measure shown to decrease in generic gravitational evolution, breaking the claimed correlation with the arrow.
- **Time-Symmetric Two-Headed Cosmos** — Reasons: the underlying laws are time-symmetric, and a Janus-point cosmos — entropy growing in both directions from a central low-entropy state — respects that symmetry instead of positing an asymmetric boundary; each side's inhabitants see a normal arrow and a beginning that is really a middle. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that the universe's global structure (for example its far future or its quantum-gravity regime) excludes a Janus configuration.
- **Fundamental Dynamical Irreversibility** — Reasons: positing irreversibility in the dynamics (collapse theories, Prigogine's programme) explains the arrow rather than relocating it to an unexplained initial condition; a boundary-condition explanation merely moves the mystery to a place where no mechanism can be demanded. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a complete derivation of all arrows from reversible dynamics plus a boundary condition the holder grants is itself explained.

#### Cosmological Initial Conditions & the Measure Problem
- **Fundamental Initial Law (Weyl Curvature / No-Boundary)** — Reasons: Penrose's Weyl Curvature Hypothesis and the Hartle–Hawking state both make the special start lawful — the initial state is selected, not stipulated; a law of initial conditions completes physics the way dynamical laws did, answering "why this start?" inside science. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either proposal's measure shown to predict a universe grossly unlike ours (for example minimal inflation) beyond repair.
- **Eternal-Inflation Multiverse Measure** — Reasons: eternal inflation is a generic output of inflationary models, and within it our low-entropy pocket is a selection effect among regions; the measure problem is an engineering difficulty of a young programme, not a refutation. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a proof that no measure can avoid Boltzmann-brain domination or youngness paradoxes across all eternal-inflation models.
- **Dynamical Bounce or Typicality Mechanism** — Reasons: a bounce converts the initial state into an outcome of prior evolution, answering the question dynamically instead of legislating it; typicality arguments similarly aim to show the observed start is what generic pre-Bang states produce. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: bounce models generically failing to deliver a low-entropy, homogeneous start without their own fine-tuned inputs.
- **Brute Empirical Boundary Condition** — Reasons: physics has always taken some boundary conditions from observation — the value of Λ, the particle masses — and the initial state may simply be one more contingent input; demanding a law of initial conditions assumes the universe owes us a reason of a kind it has not promised elsewhere. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a candidate initial law that predicts, rather than accommodates, features of the observed start.

#### Time
- **Block Universe / Eternalism** — Reasons: special relativity's relativity of simultaneity leaves no universal present to flow, and the block is the ontology that takes Minkowski geometry at face value; every successful physical theory since 1905 has been written in the block's language. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an empirically forced preferred foliation — a detected universal present — in some confirmed theory.
- **Growing Block** — Reasons: the growing block keeps relativity's past-fixity while preserving a real distinction between the settled past and the open future, matching both physics and experience; an objective edge of becoming gives quantum measurement and free action a place to happen. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a formulation problem — the "how fast does the block grow, and in whose frame?" question — proving unanswerable in principle rather than merely awkward.
- **Emergent Thermodynamic Time** — Reasons: in quantum gravity the Wheeler–DeWitt equation is timeless, and thermal-time constructions recover flow from statistical states — time as thermodynamics is a derivation, not a metaphor; the view aligns with emergent spacetime generally: geometry and time stand or fall together. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a fundamental theory in which time is basic and thermodynamics derived, reversing the claimed direction of explanation.
- **Presentism / Dynamic Time** — Reasons: becoming is the most directly experienced feature of reality, and a metaphysics that denies it to save a diagram has inverted the evidential order; neo-Lorentzian and dynamical readings show presentism can be made consistent with relativistic physics at acceptable cost. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that every presentist reconstruction of relativity smuggles in the block it claims to replace.

#### Cosmic Inflation vs. Bouncing & Cyclic Cosmologies
- **Inflation Is Established Pillar** — Reasons: inflation predicted a near-scale-invariant, slightly red-tilted, Gaussian, adiabatic perturbation spectrum in a spatially flat universe before precision CMB data confirmed each feature; that is the profile of an established pillar, not a speculation. Statistic: Planck measured the scalar spectral index as n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042, matching inflation's generic prediction of a small red tilt (Planck Collaboration, Astronomy & Astrophysics, 2020). What would move a holder: a confirmed primordial signal — non-Gaussianity shape, isocurvature, or B-mode pattern — that inflation cannot produce but a bounce predicts.
- **Inflation Promising Yet Unfinished** — Reasons: the fit is real but so are the open wounds — eternal inflation's measure problem, the unobserved B-modes, and the model's sensitivity to Planck-scale physics; calling it unfinished is a progress report, not a dismissal. Statistic: the BICEP/Keck bound limited primordial tensor modes to r < 0.036 at 95% confidence (BICEP/Keck Collaboration, Physical Review Letters, 2021). What would move a holder: detection of primordial B-modes (promoting it toward pillar) or a rival matching the spectrum with fewer assumptions.
- **Bouncing / Cyclic Alternatives Preferable** — Reasons: bounces avoid the initial singularity and the trans-Planckian and measure problems inflation creates, and ekpyrotic/cyclic models generate the observed spectrum from a contracting phase; a theory that needs no beginning is explanatory progress even before it wins on data. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a primordial B-mode detection at levels cyclic models exclude, or a proof that bounces generically fail stability or entropy requirements.

#### Interpreting the Hubble Tension (H₀)
- **Unresolved Astrophysical Systematics** — Reasons: the local distance ladder is a chain of calibrations, and a multi-sigma discrepancy at the end of a long chain is, historically, usually a link's error; independent ladders (tip of the red giant branch) have landed between the two camps, as systematics would predict. Statistic: the SH0ES measurement gave H₀ = 73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc against Planck's 67.4 ± 0.5 (Riess et al., Astrophysical Journal Letters, 2022; Planck Collaboration, 2020). What would move a holder: multiple fully independent local methods converging on the high value with disjoint systematics.
- **New Early-Universe Physics (Early Dark Energy / Neutrinos)** — Reasons: early dark energy shrinks the sound horizon, raising the CMB-inferred H₀ toward the local value while preserving the CMB fit — a targeted fix at the epoch the inference depends on; extra relativistic species act similarly and are independently motivated. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: early-dark-energy models failing the combined CMB + large-scale-structure fit as data tighten, or a laboratory/observational exclusion of the required field.
- **Late-Time Dark Energy or Modified Gravity** — Reasons: the tension is between early inference and late measurement, so a late-time change — evolving dark energy or modified gravity — is the direct reading of the discrepancy's location; hints of evolving w from recent BAO data point the same way. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: late-time probes (supernovae, BAO, weak lensing) jointly excluding any expansion history that reconciles the two H₀ values.

#### Dark Matter Theory Landscape
- **WIMPs & Thermal Dark Sectors** — Reasons: thermal freeze-out yields the observed relic abundance for weak-scale masses and couplings — the "WIMP miracle" — from cosmology's most standard machinery; the candidate class is falsifiable and being falsified or confirmed by direct detection on a definite schedule. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: direct-detection experiments reaching the neutrino fog with no signal across the miracle's parameter space.
- **Ultralight Wave / Fuzzy Axions** — Reasons: ultralight bosons solve the small-scale puzzles of cold dark matter — cusps, missing satellites — by wave dynamics with kiloparsec de Broglie wavelengths; the QCD axion simultaneously solves the strong CP problem, two problems for one particle. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: Lyman-α forest and substructure data excluding the fuzzy mass window, or haloscope exclusion of the QCD axion's motivated band.
- **Primordial Black Holes (PBHs)** — Reasons: PBHs require no new particle physics — only general relativity and early-universe density fluctuations — and remain viable as all or part of the dark matter in several mass windows; they would also seed supermassive black holes, an explanatory bonus no particle offers. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: microlensing, CMB and gravitational-wave constraints jointly closing the remaining mass windows.
- **Self-Interacting & Complex Dark Sectors** — Reasons: the visible sector is complex — forces, bound states, chemistry — and assuming the dark sector is one sterile particle is the unexamined extrapolation; self-interactions of the size galactic structure hints at would explain core/cusp diversity that collisionless models strain at. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: cluster and halo-shape constraints squeezing the self-interaction cross-section below what the galactic anomalies require.

### 3.4 Emergence & Information

#### Micro-macro
- **Fundamental Reductionism** — Reasons: every macroscopic law yet understood has yielded, in principle, to microphysical derivation, and chemistry's reduction to physics is the standing example; autonomous levels have repeatedly turned out to be convenient summaries of a deeper single story. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a macroscopic law proven underivable from microphysics in principle, not merely in practice.
- **Autonomous Emergence** — Reasons: Anderson's "More Is Different" stands — at each scale, entirely new laws appear (superconductivity, rigidity, life) whose concepts are not in the microscopic vocabulary; condensed matter's Nobel-grade successes were won by taking the macro level as its own science. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: derivations of the flagship emergent laws from microphysics that preserve, rather than replace, their explanatory content — showing autonomy was only convenience.
- **Duality Equivalence** — Reasons: AdS/CFT and its kin show micro and macro (bulk and boundary) descriptions can be exactly equivalent, making "which is fundamental?" a question about the map, not the territory; dualities have repeatedly dissolved priority disputes by exhibiting both sides as one theory. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a case where one side of a claimed duality demonstrably contains physics the other cannot express.
- **Emergent Organizing Laws Are Fundamental** — Reasons: principles like the renormalization group, universality and protected topological order govern what microphysics can even do at scale, functioning as laws about laws; Laughlin and Pines' case is that the organizing principles of the quantum protectorate are as fundamental as the Standard Model — and more relevant to most of nature. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an organizing principle shown to be a mere corollary of microscopic law with no independent constraining power.

#### Information
- **Fundamental ("It from Bit")** — Reasons: Wheeler's programme keeps scoring — black-hole entropy counts information, holography bounds information by area, and quantum theory itself is most cleanly axiomatized in information terms; if every it is derived from bits answering yes/no questions, physics' two revolutions (quantum and information) are one. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a fundamental theory in which information is demonstrably bookkeeping over a prior material substrate, with no information-theoretic constraint doing real work.
- **Information as structure** — Reasons: information-theoretic structure (entropy, correlation, mutual information) is objectively in the world — it constrains physics as thermodynamics shows — without needing to be a substance things are made of; the middle position avoids both Wheeler's mysticism and the bookkeeping view's blindness to real constraints. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either a derivation of spacetime from information promoting it to substance, or a reduction showing structural facts add nothing beyond material ones.
- **Derived** — Reasons: information is always information about something, carried by physical degrees of freedom — Landauer's principle itself says information is physical because it rides on matter and energy, not the reverse; no experiment has ever detected a bit without a substrate. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a physical law stated purely in information terms, with no material formulation, that makes a confirmed novel prediction.

#### Theory status
- **Realism** — Reasons: the no-miracles argument — it would be a miracle if theories' novel predictions (the Higgs boson, gravitational waves) succeeded were their entities not real; realism is the working assumption behind every search for a predicted particle. Statistic: the Higgs boson was discovered at about 125 GeV by ATLAS and CMS in 2012, 48 years after its prediction (ATLAS and CMS Collaborations, Physics Letters B, 2012). What would move a holder: a mature underdetermination result showing empirically equivalent theories with incompatible ontologies that no future evidence could separate.
- **Structural Realism** — Reasons: theory change preserves structure (Fresnel's equations survive inside Maxwell's) while discarding entities (the ether), so structure is the part history vindicates; structural realism is realism calibrated by the pessimistic induction instead of refuted by it. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a major theory transition in which even the mathematical structure was discarded wholesale.
- **Instrumentalism** — Reasons: a century of interpretation disputes with zero empirical resolution suggests theories' surplus structure is scaffolding, and prediction is the deliverable; instrumentalism never has to retract an ontology when theories change, because it never bought one. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an ontological commitment of a theory being directly confirmed as such — not merely its predictions succeeding.

#### Philosophical Meaning of the Renormalization Group
- **Technical Recipe for Removing Infinities** — Reasons: the RG began as a device for organizing divergences and resumming logarithms, and its calculational use needs no philosophy; inflating a computational technique into a worldview risks mistaking the tool for the terrain. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an RG-based explanation of a phenomenon — universality across unrelated materials, say — that the holder agrees no mere recipe should have been able to give.
- **Scale-Flow of Effective Descriptions** — Reasons: Wilson's RG literally defines what a theory at a scale is — integrating out degrees of freedom flows one effective description into another; this is the working content behind EFT everywhere in this skin, and it is already a substantial claim about nature's layering. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that the flow picture fails — a scale at which no effective description exists — or a derivation promoting RG from framework to law.
- **Deepest Law of Universality & Autonomy** — Reasons: universality — fluids and magnets sharing critical exponents because microscopic details wash out — is explained by RG flow and by nothing else; RG is why autonomous sciences are possible at all, making it the deepest known fact about the relation between levels. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a universality class explained without RG flow, or a failure of RG to predict an exponent it claims to govern.

#### Symmetry Breaking vs. Topological Order
- **Exact Symmetry & Spontaneous Breaking** — Reasons: Landau's paradigm organized a century of phase physics — ferromagnetism, superconductivity, the Higgs mechanism — under one scheme of symmetries and their breaking; its concepts remain the working language in which most new phases are first described. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the centre of phase physics shifting decisively to phases the paradigm cannot state, with Landau language surviving only as history.
- **Long-Range Entanglement & Topological Order** — Reasons: fractional quantum Hall states and spin liquids exhibit order — ground-state degeneracy, fractional excitations, long-range entanglement — that no local order parameter detects; topological order is a genuinely new kind of order, discovered, not relabelled. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a generalized symmetry framework fully absorbing topological order into the symmetry paradigm, showing the rivalry was one framework maturing.

#### Thermalization vs. Many-Body Localization (MBL) & Quantum Scars
- **Generic Interacting Systems Always Thermalize** — Reasons: the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis explains thermal behaviour from individual eigenstates and has passed extensive numerical tests; apparent exceptions have repeatedly fallen to longer times, larger sizes, or avalanche arguments. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an experiment or proof exhibiting stable non-thermal behaviour in a generic interacting system at infinite time in the thermodynamic limit.
- **Prethermal & Scarred Exceptions** — Reasons: quantum scars and prethermal plateaus are experimentally real — the 51-atom Rydberg simulator's revivals made non-thermal dynamics famous — yet may all be long-lived transients rather than phases; the middle position credits the phenomena without over-claiming their eternity. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either the exceptions' decay being demonstrated at accessible times (demoting them to transients) or their stabilization into a true phase.
- **True Stable Non-Ergodic MBL Phases Exist** — Reasons: strong-disorder systems exhibit local integrals of motion (l-bits), area-law eigenstates and logarithmic entanglement growth — the full signature list of a distinct phase; perturbative stability proofs exist in one dimension, and a phase of matter that refuses to thermalize is a resource (a quantum memory) as well as a discovery. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a rigorous avalanche argument, confirmed numerically at scale, showing any thermal inclusion destabilizes MBL in the thermodynamic limit.

#### Quantum Thermodynamics & Microscopic Second Law
- **Resource-Theoretic Quantum Thermodynamics** — Reasons: resource theories convert thermodynamics into exact single-shot statements — one-shot free energies, catalytic second laws — valid for the small quantum systems modern experiments actually build; the framework unifies thermodynamics with quantum information's native language. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a microscopic thermodynamic phenomenon the resource framework cannot state but fluctuation relations capture exactly.
- **Fluctuation Theorems & Stochastic Thermodynamics** — Reasons: Jarzynski and Crooks relations are exact equalities governing individual fluctuating trajectories, experimentally verified from single molecules to quantum dots; they explain irreversibility statistically without coarse-graining decrees. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a regime — strongly quantum, coherent, single-shot — where fluctuation theorems fall silent and resource-theoretic laws take over completely.

#### Quantum Chaos & Fast Scrambling Bounds
- **Centrality of Chaos Bounds (λ_L ≤ 2πT) & OTOCs in Fundamental Physics** — Reasons: the Maldacena–Shenker–Stanford bound on chaos is saturated by black holes and by the SYK model, tying a quantum-information quantity to gravity's extremal objects; Planckian dissipation in strange metals hints the same bound operates in real materials, making it a rare bridge across fundamental physics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a physical system violating the bound, or scrambling physics proving irrelevant to the black-hole information problem it was built for.

### 3.5 Symmetry & Fine-Tuning

#### Symmetry
- **Fundamental** — Reasons: Wigner's classification made particles themselves representations of symmetry groups, and gauge symmetry dictates the Standard Model's interactions almost uniquely; symmetry principles (Noether's theorem) generate the conservation laws on which all else rests. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a fundamental phenomenon demonstrably governed by no symmetry principle, where symmetry language adds nothing.
- **Broken** — Reasons: a perfectly symmetric world would be featureless — no masses (electroweak breaking), no structure (inflationary perturbations), no arrow of complexity; the phenomena live in the breaking, and the Higgs mechanism is the paradigm of physics happening where symmetry fails. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the deepest open problems (flavour, hierarchy) yielding to pure symmetry arguments with breaking as bookkeeping.

#### Fine-Tuning
- **Naturalness Dogma** — Reasons: naturalness has a track record — it predicted the charm quark's mass scale and the positron's style of reasoning repeatedly paid; parameters tuned to one part in 10^30 (the Higgs mass against Planck-scale corrections) are, by the field's own historical standards, a symptom of missing mechanism. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstrated case where an extreme tuning is explained environmentally with independent evidence for the environment.
- **Naturalness as a heuristic** — Reasons: naturalness has both scored (charm) and missed (the cosmological constant, weak-scale SUSY), which is exactly the profile of a heuristic — weight it, don't worship it; discarding it entirely would throw away real Bayesian evidence about where new physics hides. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: naturalness-guided searches either striking (promoting it) or exhausting their motivated space empty (demoting it).
- **Acceptance of Fine-Tuning** — Reasons: parameters are what measurement says they are, and "tuning" smuggles in a prior over values nobody has justified; the cosmological constant already forced the field to live with one enormous tuning, possibly environmental — the Higgs may simply be another. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a natural mechanism for the Higgs mass confirmed experimentally, showing the tuning was indeed a clue.

#### Anthropic principle
- **Accept** — Reasons: Weinberg's anthropic estimate of Λ remains the only successful prediction-shaped reasoning about its value; observers can only find themselves where observers are possible, and ignoring a known selection effect is bad statistics, not rigour. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a non-anthropic derivation of Λ and the other tuned parameters from a unique vacuum.
- **Weak anthropic only** — Reasons: selection effects are undeniable bookkeeping — we observe from a habitable universe — but strong anthropic reasoning explains parameters by our existence and thereby explains nothing testable; the weak version is a correction factor, the strong a sedative. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an anthropic argument yielding a confirmed, risky prediction beyond Λ, upgrading the strong version's status.
- **Reject** — Reasons: anthropic reasoning is unfalsifiable as practised — any observed value becomes "explained" by observers requiring it; a science that accepts it stops looking for mechanisms precisely where mechanisms have historically been found. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the landscape-plus-selection framework producing a statistical prediction (a distribution over parameters) that observation confirms.

#### Beauty
- **Aesthetic & Structural Beauty (Dirac)** — Reasons: Dirac's equation, general relativity and the gauge principle were each beauty-led and data-vindicated; mathematical depth has been the most reliable compass at the frontier, where data is scarcest — Dirac's dictum is a summary of the field's own history. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a frontier advance achieved by an avowedly ugly, data-forced construction that beauty-guided rivals missed.
- **Strict Falsifiability (Popper)** — Reasons: beauty is culturally variable and historically treacherous — steady-state cosmology and weak-scale SUSY were both beautiful; falsifiability is the one criterion that has never licensed a degenerating programme to call itself physics indefinitely. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a beautiful, initially untestable theory later confirmed in detail — string theory's vindication would be the type case.

#### Black hole information
- **Unitary Page Curve via Quantum Extremal Islands** — Reasons: semiclassical gravity calculations since 2019 reproduce the Page curve — entropy rising then falling as unitarity requires — via island contributions, using only replica wormholes in the gravitational path integral; the paradox's mathematics now answers itself within gravity's own rules. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that island calculations fail for realistic (non-supersymmetric, four-dimensional, evaporating) black holes.
- **Firewalls or Fuzzballs Modify Horizon** — Reasons: the AMPS argument shows unitarity, equivalence-principle smoothness and effective field theory cannot all hold, and the horizon is where something must give; string theory's fuzzball programme independently replaces the horizon with structure, counting microstates geometrically. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an island-style resolution that preserves a smooth horizon for realistic black holes without hidden assumptions the holder rejects.
- **Remnants or Baby Universes** — Reasons: storing the information in a Planck-mass remnant or a pinched-off baby universe preserves both unitarity (globally) and the smooth horizon; the information is not destroyed, merely sequestered where Hawking's argument cannot follow it. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the standard objections — infinite remnant species, unbounded pair production — being made rigorous against all remnant models.
- **Genuine Information Loss** — Reasons: Hawking's original conclusion may simply be right — quantum gravity may be fundamentally non-unitary, and insisting otherwise to save a principle learned from non-gravitational physics begs the question; information loss would be a discovery about nature, not a defect of the theory. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the island/Page-curve calculations being established for realistic black holes to the holder's standard of proof.

#### Local Lagrangians vs. On-Shell S-Matrix & Bootstrap
- **Local Lagrangians & Path Integrals** — Reasons: the Lagrangian framework built the Standard Model and computes everything from g−2 to collider cross-sections; fields and locality encode causality structurally, and abandoning them for amplitudes trades a theory for a list of its outputs. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an amplitude/bootstrap method computing a quantity (a non-perturbative spectrum, a finite-coupling result) that Lagrangian methods cannot reach.
- **Complementary Dual Languages** — Reasons: the two frameworks keep proving equivalent where both apply, and each has domains of clear superiority — Lagrangians for bound states and phases, amplitudes for scattering simplicity; bilingualism is what the evidence supports, and monoglot programmes keep rediscovering the other language's results. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: one framework subsuming the other's domain entirely, ending the complementarity in fact.
- **On-Shell Amplitudes & Bootstrap First** — Reasons: gauge redundancy means most of a Lagrangian's content is unphysical bookkeeping, while on-shell amplitudes are the observables themselves; the amplituhedron and bootstrap programmes derive locality and unitarity from deeper principles instead of assuming them — the direction a fundamental formulation should run. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a structural result — bound states, SSB, confinement — that on-shell methods provably cannot formulate.

#### Ontology of Quantum Field Theory
- **Effective Quantum Fields** — Reasons: fields with a physical cutoff are what every working calculation uses, and their ontology is modest — operator-valued distributions defined where the EFT is valid; particle and vacuum phenomena (Casimir forces, Lamb shift) are field phenomena before they are anything else. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: Haag-style results being sharpened into a demonstration that field ontology misleads even within the EFT's own domain.
- **Nets of Local Operator Algebras** — Reasons: the Haag–Kastler framework is the only formulation of QFT with theorems — Reeh–Schlieder, Bisognano–Wichmann — deep enough to derive thermal (Unruh) effects from algebra structure; particles and fields come and go with frames and interactions, but the net of local algebras is invariant content. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the algebraic programme failing to recover the Standard Model's working content where field language succeeds.
- **On-Shell Scattering Amplitudes** — Reasons: the S-matrix is the complete list of what QFT predicts for scattering, and bootstrap results show it can be characterized without fields at all; off-shell fields differ between gauges and formulations, which is the mark of representation, not ontology. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a physical phenomenon — vacuum structure, bound states — requiring off-shell field content in principle.
- **Primitive Particles or Flashes** — Reasons: measurement outcomes are localized events, and an ontology of particles (Bohm) or flashes (GRW) says what exists in spacetime without interpretive remainder; field ontology must still explain why the world presents as events, which beable ontologies do natively. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a relativistic beable theory failing where field ontology succeeds, in a regime both claim.

#### Effective Field Theory vs. UV Completion
- **EFT Is All Empirical Physics Needs** — Reasons: decoupling is a theorem-shaped fact — heavy physics leaves only renormalizable footprints plus suppressed corrections — so every experiment ever done is EFT business; UV completion is a want of theorists, not a need of data. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an observation violating decoupling — low-energy data that only a specific UV completion can explain.
- **EFT + Swampland UV–IR Constraints** — Reasons: not every consistent-looking EFT completes into quantum gravity — the Swampland programme's conjectures (no global symmetries, weak gravity) are supported by every known string example; UV–IR constraints give EFT users real information from quantum gravity without the full theory. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a controlled counterexample — an EFT violating a major Swampland conjecture yet provably UV-completable.
- **Complete UV Theory Required** — Reasons: EFT by construction cannot answer why its parameters hold, whether its vacuum is stable, or what happens at its cutoff — the questions this whole skin asks; settling for EFT is settling for a permanent horizon on explanation. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a proof that the UV questions have no physical content — that every observable is EFT-computable in principle.

#### Carving Out UV Physics: Explicit Models vs. Bootstrap Axioms
- **Explicit UV-Complete Models** — Reasons: string theory's concrete constructions delivered the field's hardest results — microstate counting, holography, mirror symmetry — because explicit models can be interrogated; axiomatic carving without models risks mapping the space of theories nobody can build. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: bootstrap bounds excluding the explicit models' shared predictions, or deriving them more cheaply.
- **Model-Independent Bootstrap & Positivity Bounds** — Reasons: unitarity, causality, analyticity and crossing carve theory space rigorously regardless of which UV model nature chose — positivity bounds already constrain real EFT coefficients; model-independence is insurance against betting the field on one speculative completion. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an explicit model making a confirmed prediction that the bounds had ruled out of the carved space.

Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a top-down model delivering a confirmed prediction before the data demanded it.

#### Mechanism of Color Confinement in QCD
- **Dual Superconductor (Monopole Condensation)** — Reasons: the dual Meissner picture gives confinement a mechanism — condense magnetic monopoles and electric flux squeezes into tubes with the observed constant tension; lattice studies in specific gauges find monopole dominance consistent with the picture. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: gauge-invariant evidence that monopole condensation is a gauge artefact rather than the mechanism.
- **Center Vortices & Topology** — Reasons: removing center vortices from lattice configurations destroys confinement and chiral symmetry breaking together, while vortex-only ensembles reproduce the string tension; topology-based mechanisms explain confinement and chiral phenomena in one stroke. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: vortex phenomenology failing a quantitative test — string tension ratios, Casimir scaling — that a rival mechanism passes.
- **Lattice & Dyson–Schwinger Numerical Reality** — Reasons: lattice QCD computes the hadron spectrum from first principles to percent accuracy — the theory's reality is what the computation exhibits, and "mechanism" talk is commentary on numerical fact; functional methods complement with analytic control of Green's functions. Statistic: lattice QCD reproduced the light-hadron spectrum with errors at the few-percent level from first principles (Dürr et al., Science, 2008). What would move a holder: an analytic mechanism deriving the lattice's numbers, promoting commentary into explanation.
- **Holographic / String Flux-Tube Duality** — Reasons: gauge/string duality geometrizes the flux tube — confinement in holographic models is the string's inability to reach the boundary, giving area law from geometry; the picture unifies confinement with the strong-coupling methods that work elsewhere. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: holographic confinement predictions (glueball spectra, tension ratios) failing against lattice values in the models' shared domain.

#### Perturbative Loops vs. Non-Perturbative Anomalies, Instantons & Resurgence
- **Precision Multi-Loop Perturbation Theory** — Reasons: perturbative calculation is where QFT touches experiment hardest — the anomalous magnetic moment and collider cross-sections at multi-loop order are physics' most precise agreements; depth here is depth in contact with data. Statistic: the electron's anomalous magnetic moment is measured and calculated to better than one part in a billion, among the most precise agreements in science (Fan et al., Physical Review Letters, 2023). What would move a holder: the precision programme stalling on a quantity that non-perturbative methods then deliver.
- **Resurgent Trans-Series Bridge** — Reasons: perturbative series diverge factorially, and resurgence theory shows the divergence encodes the non-perturbative saddles — the series knows about instantons; trans-series unify the two regimes into one exact object, dissolving the rivalry this topic poses. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a system where resurgent reconstruction provably fails to recover known non-perturbative physics.
- **Non-Perturbative Anomalies & Dualities First** — Reasons: 't Hooft anomaly matching constrains strongly coupled dynamics exactly, without solving it, and dualities (Seiberg duality, gauge/gravity) have repeatedly delivered strong-coupling truth that loops cannot approach; the deepest results of the last half-century are disproportionately non-perturbative. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: perturbative methods reaching a flagship strong-coupling result first, reversing the track record.

#### Generalized Higher-Form & Non-Invertible Symmetries
- **Over-Formalized Repackaging** — Reasons: much of the generalized-symmetry literature re-derives known results (selection rules, 't Hooft anomalies) in heavier language; a paradigm shift should announce itself with predictions, and the field's ratio of formalism to new physics remains high. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a confirmed phenomenon — a phase, a constraint on real materials — that only the generalized framework found.
- **Sharp Tool for Confinement & Phases** — Reasons: higher-form symmetries state confinement itself as a symmetry question (unbroken one-form symmetry = confinement), converting folklore into criterion; the tool has classified phases and anomalies that the old language handled case by case. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the tool's classifications proving empty for realistic (non-supersymmetric, finite-density) systems.
- **Revolutionary New Paradigm of Symmetry** — Reasons: Wigner's paradigm tied symmetry to groups acting on points; symmetries acting on lines and surfaces, and non-invertible fusion rules, genuinely exceed that frame — SymTFT organizes all of a theory's generalized symmetries in one bulk theory; when the definition of symmetry itself widens, "paradigm shift" is the accurate term. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a reduction showing every generalized symmetry is an ordinary symmetry of an enlarged but conventional theory.

#### Integrability vs. Generic Non-Integrable Chaos in QFT
- **Exact Integrability & Bethe Ansatz** — Reasons: integrable models are the only interacting QFTs solved exactly at all couplings — planar N=4 SYM's spectrum is the standing monument — and exact solutions anchor every approximation scheme; physics has always advanced from its solvable islands (Kepler, hydrogen) outward. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: integrable methods failing to export any technique or intuition to the generic theories that constitute most of nature.
- **Integrable Anchor + Perturbative Breaking** — Reasons: starting from an integrable point and perturbing away combines exact control with generic reach — the strategy behind much of condensed matter's success with perturbed critical points; pure integrability is measure-zero, pure chaos is intractable, and the working frontier is the bridge. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: integrability-breaking expansions diverging uselessly in the flagship cases, or generic methods maturing enough to skip the anchor.
- **Embrace Generic Non-Integrable Dynamics** — Reasons: nature's generic systems are chaotic, and intuition trained on measure-zero integrable miracles (infinite conserved charges, no thermalization) actively misleads about them; the field's centre of mass should sit where the phenomena are — thermalization, transport, scrambling. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an integrable model delivering the decisive insight into a generic phenomenon (a transport coefficient, a thermalization mechanism) that chaotic methods missed.

#### Strong CP Problem & Axion vs. Anthropic/Massless-Quark Explanations
- **Peccei–Quinn QCD Axion Solution** — Reasons: the PQ mechanism promotes θ̄ to a dynamical field whose potential is minimized at zero — a mechanism, not a coincidence — and predicts a particle, the axion, that experiments are actively hunting; the axion would simultaneously be a leading dark-matter candidate. Statistic: the neutron electric dipole moment limit, |d_n| < 1.8 × 10⁻²⁶ e·cm, bounds θ̄ below roughly 10⁻¹⁰ (Abel et al., Physical Review Letters, 2020). What would move a holder: haloscopes and helioscopes excluding the QCD axion across its motivated mass–coupling band.
- **Nelson–Barr / Spontaneous CP Violation** — Reasons: Nelson–Barr constructions make CP exact in the fundamental theory and break it spontaneously in a way that keeps θ̄ small by structure, without a new light particle that forty years of searches have not found; the solution also speaks to the origin of the observed CP violation in the weak sector. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: discovery of the QCD axion (making the alternative unnecessary) or a no-go result against natural Nelson–Barr model-building.
- **Re-Examine Topological Vacuum & UV Framing** — Reasons: the strong CP problem presupposes that θ̄ is a UV-sensitive parameter like any other, but θ-vacua, large gauge transformations and UV/IR mixing make that framing contestable; some "problems" in this skin's history dissolved when their framing was re-examined rather than solved. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a consensus derivation showing the standard framing is forced, returning the problem to the solvers.

### 3.6 The Life of a Theorist

#### Theorist's method
- **Analytic Pen & Chalk** — Reasons: hand derivation builds the intuition that tells a theorist which of a computer's thousand outputs matters; the field's canonical insights (Feynman's diagrams, Penrose's twistor diagrams) are chalk-native, and analytic control is what separates understanding from evaluation. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a landmark result obtained purely computationally that no analytic approach could have found or explained.
- **Automated Symbolic Computation** — Reasons: modern multi-loop amplitudes and tensor calculations exceed human stamina by orders of magnitude, and refusing automation is refusing the frontier's actual problems; computer algebra also checks the hand work, catching the sign errors chalk is famous for. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: automated methods producing volume without insight across a sustained period, while analytic work keeps delivering the concepts.

#### Mathematics
- **Discovered** — Reasons: mathematics repeatedly anticipates physics — Riemannian geometry awaited Einstein, group theory awaited particle physics — which is inexplicable if we invent it to fit; independent cultures converging on the same theorems suggests a territory, not a convention. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstration that the "unreasonable effectiveness" is selection bias — that invented systems mostly fail and we remember the fits.
- **Invented, then discovered** — Reasons: we plainly choose the axioms and notations (invention), yet their consequences surprise and resist us (discovery) — non-Euclidean geometry was invented as a game and discovered to be physics; the hybrid matches the phenomenology of doing mathematics better than either pure view. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a case forcing the choice — consequences shown to be as conventional as axioms (toward Invented) or axioms shown forced (toward Discovered).
- **Invented** — Reasons: mathematics is a human cultural product with a history — zero, negatives and proof itself were each resisted as illegitimate before adoption; its fit to physics is engineered by selecting, from the vast invented stock, the fragments that model well. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a mathematical truth demonstrably independent of any human-chosen framework — the same content in every possible formalism.

#### Research focus
- **Theory** — Reasons: deep structure outlives phenomena — thermodynamics' data is forgotten, its laws are not — and the fundamental framework is where a single insight reorganizes everything downstream; phenomenology without theory is curve-fitting with better vocabulary. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a sustained period in which the framework stalls and all progress is phenomenological, on the Standard Model's own historical pattern.
- **Theory at the phenomena** — Reasons: the field's golden episodes — BCS, the quark model, inflation — happened where deep structure met insistent phenomena; phenomena discipline theory's imagination, and theory gives phenomena their meaning, and the seam is where both are strongest. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: either pure theory or pure phenomenology decisively out-producing the seam across a generation.
- **Phenomenology** — Reasons: models that predict what experiments will see are the only theory-products the rest of science can spend; in an era of LHC, CMB and gravitational-wave data, the binding constraint is interpretation of phenomena, not shortage of frameworks. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a framework-first breakthrough (a confirmed fundamental theory) that reorganizes phenomenology from above.

#### Working style
- **Solitude** — Reasons: deep theoretical work requires uninterrupted hours no seminar schedule survives, and the canonical figures (Newton at Woolsthorpe, Einstein's patent-office years) did their deepest work alone; collaboration optimizes for the average idea, solitude for the extreme one. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that the field's landmark results are now predominantly team products in a way that reflects necessity, not just sociology.
- **Solo stretches in a team** — Reasons: collaboration supplies criticism, skills and morale while protected solitude supplies depth — the mixture is how most working theorists actually produce; each mode corrects the other's characteristic failure (groupthink; unchallenged error). Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a demonstrated optimum at either extreme across comparable research programmes.
- **Collaboration** — Reasons: modern problems cross specialisms no single mind holds — a gravitational-wave template needs relativity, numerics and statistics at once; the seminar and the shared draft are error-correction machinery, and physics' error rate drops in company. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a landmark solo result in a domain the holder agrees had become team-shaped.

#### Non-Empirical Theory Assessment vs. Strict Falsifiability
- **Strict Popperian Falsifiability** — Reasons: demarcation by falsifiability is the only criterion that has ever retired a degenerating programme against its will; non-empirical "confirmation" is confirmation by the community's prior taste, which is what falsifiability was invented to overrule. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a theory confirmed non-empirically and later vindicated by experiment in detail — the type case the position says cannot occur.
- **Cautious Heuristic Priors** — Reasons: scientists unavoidably use coherence, track record and elegance as weak evidence when allocating effort; making the priors explicit and small is more honest than pretending they are zero or letting them grow into confirmation. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that heuristic priors systematically misrank theories against later experimental verdicts.
- **Bayesian Non-Empirical Confirmation** — Reasons: Dawid's arguments are formally sound — if a theory is the only one found by a competent search satisfying strong constraints, that meta-level fact raises its probability as any evidence does; string theory's unexpected coherence (dualities, black-hole counting) is data about the theory's relation to truth, not mere taste. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a case where no-alternatives reasoning was satisfied and the theory was nonetheless experimentally refuted.

#### Mathematical Rigor in Quantum Field Theory
- **Physical Path-Integral Intuition** — Reasons: the physicist's QFT predicts the most precise numbers in science while its rigorous counterpart cannot yet construct an interacting 4D theory — the intuition is evidently tracking something real; rigor follows insight historically (calculus waited two centuries for ε–δ) without the insight being wrong meanwhile. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a case where path-integral intuition gave a wrong physical answer that rigor caught in advance.
- **Selective Rigorous Anchors** — Reasons: full axiomatization of everything is a poor allocation, but load-bearing claims — unitarity, anomaly cancellation, entropy bounds — deserve proof-grade support; anchoring selectively buys most of rigor's safety at a fraction of its cost. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: an unanchored load-bearing claim collapsing (tightening the standard) or anchors repeatedly proving redundant (loosening it).
- **Axiomatic & Constructive QFT Mandate** — Reasons: Haag's theorem shows the physicist's interaction picture is mathematically inconsistent as usually stated — intuition has already been caught out once at the foundations; the Clay Yang–Mills problem (existence and mass gap) marks the debt officially: a theory this central should exist rigorously. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: constructive QFT failing permanently on 4D gauge theory while physics' predictions remain exact — evidence the mandate asks for something nature does not contain.

#### Toy Models vs. Realistic Complexity & Analytic vs. Symbolic Tools
- **Solvable Toy Models (Ising, SYK, 2D CFT) ↔ Realistic Messy Systems** — Reasons: on the toy-model side, solvable systems (Ising, SYK, 2D CFT) are where mechanisms are isolated and proven — SYK taught scrambling, Ising taught criticality; on the realistic side, nature's actual systems are messy, and toy-trained intuition has repeatedly failed on contact with disorder, interactions and finite size. This is one axis of a switchbox: affirming either end, both, or neither is coherent, and the two axes cross independently. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a flagship question settled decisively from one side — a toy model predicting a messy system's behaviour quantitatively, or a messy-system result no toy anticipated.
- **Pen-and-Paper Analytical ↔ Computer Algebra & Numerical Assist** — Reasons: on the pen-and-paper side, analytic work is where understanding lives and where errors are owned; on the computational side, the frontier's calculations (multi-loop, lattice, numerical relativity) are simply beyond unaided hands, and assist tools extend rather than replace thought. This is the switchbox's second axis, independent of the first: a holder may pair toy models with computation, or realistic systems with chalk. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a sustained shift in where the field's decisive results originate along this axis.

#### Preferred Theoretical Aesthetic
- **Symmetry & Invariance** — Reasons: invariance principles have the deepest track record in theory choice — from relativity to gauge theory, symmetry has been nature's own organizing device; a theory maximizing symmetry maximizes contact with that record. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the next fundamental advance arriving with less symmetry than the theories it replaces.
- **Analytical Solvability** — Reasons: a solvable theory can be fully known — its spectrum, its limits, its surprises — and exact results are the only theory-statements that never need revision; solvability converts speculation into knowledge one model at a time. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: solvable models' lessons repeatedly failing to transfer to the unsolvable theories nature actually uses.
- **Minimal Free Parameters** — Reasons: every free parameter is an unexplained fact, so parameter economy measures explanatory depth directly — the Standard Model's ~19 parameters are its confession list; theories that predict numbers others must input are strictly better when both fit. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a parameter-rich theory (a landscape sector, say) making confirmed predictions a minimal rival cannot.
- **Unexpected Cross-Domain Duality** — Reasons: dualities are the discoveries nobody's prior expected — gauge/gravity, wave/particle, electric/magnetic — and each reorganized two fields at once; a duality's surprise is evidence it reflects structure rather than taste. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a celebrated duality proving to be approximate bookkeeping with no exact content.
- **Sharp Experimental Predictivity** — Reasons: a theory that risks itself on numbers can be wrong, and only such theories can be right in the way physics honours; predictivity is the aesthetic that is also a methodology, disciplining all the others. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: a predictively timid theory being confirmed as the fundamental one, with sharp rivals excluded.

#### Research Strategy: Mainstream Program vs. Independent Contrarian
- **Advance Dominant Mainstream Program** — Reasons: flagship programmes concentrate talent, tools and scrutiny, and cumulative progress inside them is real — precision QFT and numerical relativity are mainstream products; most contrarian bets fail, and the mainstream is where a career's expected contribution is highest. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the flagship programme's own leaders conceding a structural dead end, or a contrarian programme delivering the field's next confirmed result.
- **Mainstream Core + Contrarian Bets** — Reasons: portfolio logic — the mainstream core keeps a theorist competent and connected while side bets buy exposure to the fat tail where revolutions live; the field's health likewise needs a majority core and a funded minority of heretics. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: evidence that split attention underperforms both pure strategies across careers.
- **Independent Unorthodox Foundations** — Reasons: after decades without experimental confirmation of the flagship's distinctive claims, the local-minimum diagnosis deserves its bet — Kuhn's structure says revolutions come from outside the paradigm's centre; independence is also the only strategy whose successes are not correlated with everyone else's failure. Statistic: No reliable statistic found. What would move a holder: the mainstream flagship delivering a confirmed, distinctive prediction, re-legitimizing the centre.

### Copenhagen interpretation — `quanta-copenhagen.svg`
The orthodox interpretation of Bohr and Heisenberg: the wavefunction is complete, measurement collapses it, and complementary descriptions are both needed and mutually exclusive. Why: it holds the §3 Copenhagen reasons — predictive completeness without surplus ontology — and the ψ-epistemic, collapse and operational-pragmatist positions that follow from them (§3, Quantum Interpretations; Wavefunction; "Shut Up and Calculate!"). Receptive to: decoherence as the account of classical appearance, and QBist belief-talk as a friendly redescription (§3, Measurement). Closed to: hidden-variable trajectories and branching worlds, because both add the unobserved machinery its whole case refuses (§3, Bell's theorem). Emblem: images/emblems/quanta-copenhagen.svg

### Many-Worlds interpretation — `quanta-many-worlds.svg`
Everett's interpretation: the wavefunction never collapses and every outcome occurs in its own branching world. Why: it holds the §3 Everettian reasons — one unitary law, no collapse postulate — with emergent branching probability, no-collapse measurement, and local branching on Bell (§3, Quantum Interpretations; Where does quantum probability come from?). Receptive to: decoherence (its mechanism for branch formation) and emergent spacetime from entanglement (§3, Does Decoherence Solve the Measurement Problem?; Spacetime). Closed to: objective collapse, because a single verified non-unitary event ends the position (§3, Measurement). Emblem: images/emblems/quanta-many-worlds.svg

### De Broglie-Bohm interpretation — `quanta-bohm.svg`
Pilot-wave theory: particles always have definite positions, guided by a real wavefunction; determinism holds, randomness is ignorance of initial positions, and nonlocality is explicit. Why: it holds the §3 pilot-wave reasons — a clear ontology of beables — with ψ-ontic wavefunction, strict determinism and non-local realism (§3, Wavefunction; Determinism; Bell's theorem). Receptive to: primitive-particle ontology in QFT and retrocausal cousins (§3, Ontology of Quantum Field Theory). Closed to: anti-realist and purely epistemic readings, because its whole point is a world that is definite whether or not anyone looks (§3, Theory status). Emblem: images/emblems/quanta-bohm.svg

### QBism — `quanta-qbism.svg`
Quantum Bayesianism: a quantum state is an agent's personal degrees of belief about the outcomes of their future actions; collapse is belief updating, not a physical process. Why: it holds the §3 QBist reasons — probability as belief everywhere in science — with ψ-epistemic states, subjective probability and the agent as observer (§3, Quantum Interpretations; Where does quantum probability come from?; What counts as an observer?). Receptive to: Copenhagen as a near-ally and operational reconstructions of quantum theory (§3, Reconstructing Quantum Theory from Operational Axioms). Closed to: ψ-ontic theorems taken at face value and any physical collapse mechanism (§3, Wavefunction). Emblem: images/emblems/quanta-qbism.svg

### GRW / Dynamical Objective Collapse Realism — `quanta-grw.svg`
GRW (Ghirardi–Rimini–Weber, 1986): collapse is a real, stochastic, observer-independent process; standard linear quantum mechanics is an approximation failing at macroscopic scales, with Penrose's gravity-linked variant and Bassi and colleagues' (2013) review of experimental tests. Why: it holds the §3 objective-collapse reasons — macroscopic superpositions are never seen because dynamics prevents them — with objective collapse probability, irreducible randomness and testability as a boast (§3, Measurement; Can your interpretation be tested?). Receptive to: fundamental dynamical irreversibility and any experiment that bounds collapse parameters (§3, Arrow of time). Closed to: no-collapse positions, whose unitary purity its experiments aim to refute (§3, Measurement). Emblem: images/emblems/quanta-grw.svg

### Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM) — `quanta-relational.svg`
Rovelli's interpretation (1996; Helgoland, 2021): states and quantities have values only relative to an interacting system; the world is a network of sparse relational events, not one absolute wavefunction. Why: it holds the §3 relational reasons — the relativization strategy that worked for simultaneity — with relational wavefunction, any-system observers and observer-relative facts (§3, Wavefunction; What counts as an observer?; Wigner's Friend). Receptive to: emergent thermodynamic time and background-independent gravity, its founder's other commitments (§3, Time; Background Independence Mandate). Closed to: absolute-facts positions and any privileged-observer doctrine (§3, Wigner's Friend). Emblem: images/emblems/quanta-relational.svg

### The String-Holographic Unifier — `quanta-string-unifier.svg`
Pursues UV unification through string theory, dualities, AdS/CFT, quantum-extremal islands and emergent entanglement geometry (Witten, Maldacena, Polchinski, Susskind, Arkani-Hamed). Why: it holds the §3 string reasons — perturbative finiteness, microstate counting, holography — with grand-unified ambition, emergent spacetime, unitary island Page curves and non-empirical confirmation (§3, The Quantum Gravity Crossroads; Black hole information; Non-Empirical Theory Assessment). ### The Background-Independent Relativist — `quanta-relativist.svg`

## 5. Gap analysis

**Positions with no archetype.** The map's poles are far wider than its 15 archetypes. No archetype primarily embodies: asymptotic safety or CDT as a programme identity (the Relativist neighbours them but is LQG-shaped); steady-state cosmology; presentism as a metaphysics-first identity; instrumentalism as a philosophy-of-science identity; Nelson–Barr strong-CP resolution; center-vortex confinement; or the Pen-and-Chalk solitary as a standalone persona (several archetypes lean analytic, none is defined by method alone). The six interpretation archetypes cover the six poles of Quantum Interpretations exactly; the gap is in programmes and methods, not interpretations.

**Thin topics.** Three topics carry a single pole — Background Independence Mandate (xqu0042), Strong-Field Gravitational-Wave Theory & Black-Hole Spectroscopy (xqu0047), Quantum Chaos & Fast Scrambling Bounds (xqu0055) — so they register affinity (negative ratings are aversion) but no disagreement of positions; the indaba can list backing for the sole pole (done in §3) but cannot locate a meeting point. The switchbox topic (xqu0065) crosses two independent axes; both-negative is its status quo, not an error.
