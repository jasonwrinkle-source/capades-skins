## 1. Domain framing

Biocapades maps where you stand on the life sciences: what drives evolution, how much of a life the genome scripts, where the boundary of life runs, and what we may do with the tools that now edit it. Biology bridges hard empirical theory and sharp bioethical choices — and deep controversies often hinge on which level of organization, gene, organism, lineage, or biosphere, you treat as primary. The domain is Life — Evolution, Genes and What It Is to Be Alive. A position in this skin is a rated stance on one of its 46 topics: for each pole a holder advocates for it, against it, or toward it with aversion (negative advocacy is aversion, not absence of a view). Holding no stance is not a position; a 0 rating is. The meter word is **perspective**: the skin measures how strongly a profile stands for each pole (advocacy, per pole) and how open it is to each topic at all (openness, per topic). Advocacy per pole and openness per topic are the inputs. Receptivity and intensity are derived, never rated: receptivity = round-half-even(openness × advocacy), computed with exact fractions, and intensity = the signed largest-|value| pole. The Scribe is an indaba / consensus process: it uses this research to show each position with its backing, name where holders meet and where they part, and ask what would let the group reach consensus. The skin's stance is the one stated in its own `about` text above: it maps the domain's live positions in one shared vocabulary, with no pole treated as the default.
